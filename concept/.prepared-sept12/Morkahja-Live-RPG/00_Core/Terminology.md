# Terminology

**Current concept through answer 202 · updated 2026-09-12.**

Consolidated concept material, with current rules and unresolved details distinguished.

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Morkahja | Existing elemental world, used here in an ancient era. Earlier transcription variants are normalized to this spelling. |
| Rokur / Embersworn | People of living flame; associated with Fyrskar Mountains. |
| Verdani / Bloomkin | Horned people of living water; associated with Evergrove. |
| Aevis / Windweavers | People of air and aether; associated with Celesterra and subterranean cities. |
| Grimnir / Stonewrought | People of stone, metal, and crystal; associated with later floating Skylands, which do not yet exist in this game’s early era. |
| Inhabitation | Temporary player control of an existing world character. |
| Trait | A binary, persistent quality; one has it or does not. |
| Birth traits | The 27 latent personal qualities: 9 from each parent and 9 from the full pool; local unmet Desires may prioritize future generation. |
| Social role | A culturally interpreted calling assigned at adulthood, granting abilities and interactions. |
| Destiny | A personal procedural story direction beginning at adulthood; only players advance it. |
| Side quest | An associated objective that supports destiny and can award an experience-related trait. |
| Node | Persistent geography with multiple replaceable current-content slots; history lives in Shards. |
| Shard | Functional situational knowledge paired with a Need; distinct from persistent personal Traits. |
| Memory well | Cultural collective-knowledge hub for contribution and relevant updates; no burden system. Cultural form varies. |
| Godly essence | The divine vitality whose exhaustion leads to judgment at Atranor. |
| Atranor | Place where the gods decide continuation or dissolution. |
| Dissolution | Loss of individual existence; also described as a return to elemental unity. |
| Corruption | Transformation through corrupt magic into a being harmful to the world. |
| Stand-in | Opted-in queued player temporarily controlling a disconnected player's character. |

“Live RPG” is the project title. The established meaning in practice is persistent multiplayer simulation with temporary inhabitation, not a requirement for live-action physical roleplay.

| Term | Current definition |
| --- | --- |
| Desire | Persistent direction paired with a Trait; replaces Longing/persistent Need. |
| Need | Situational requirement paired with a Shard. |
| Shard | Functional situational knowledge; no capacity or weight. Personal stale information is replaced when newer knowledge is received. |
| Baseline embodiment | Shared humanoid actions plus elemental capabilities, modified by personal traits, role, equipment, and state. |
| World node | Persistent geography with multiple current content slots; no local historical diary. |
| Collective memory | Layered historical shards; newest relevant known information serves current Needs. |
| Injury | Broad functional state restricting actions; incapacity opens alternative interactions. |
| Engagement | Combat lock with game-managed facing and positioning; disengage for independent movement. |

The earlier “laying down memory to lighten oneself” concept is superseded by simple knowledge collection and contribution. Desires and Shards are now distinct named parts of the character model, rather than undefined alternatives to Traits.

## Refined terms

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Youth | Earliest playable phase, with full elemental baseline and hidden personal qualities still emerging through participation. |
| Coming-of-age rite | Final development quest/ritual revealing role and beginning adulthood and destiny. |
| Divine Orientation | Intermittent divine signs toward the current useful step, separate from personal knowledge. |
| Discovered interaction | Permanently documented character action, with current blockers shown and no pre-discovery hints. |
| Craft quality | More applicable output traits and compatibility, not a vertical item level. |
| Community task | Continuously simulation-generated suitable work accepted through a cultural interaction. |

“Atrana” in later speech is treated as the already-established Atranor, not a newly introduced place. No separate Quirks subsystem is established by a passing mention in the panel description.




## Additional and refined terms

- **Destiny Crest:** one branching-out and reconverging section of an adult life, followed by further Crests.
- **Destiny Knot:** a Crest's communal ritual culmination, with lasting growth and continued life beyond it.
- **Reproduction Destiny:** each parent's temporary progress-driven quest structure for nurturing a new Grimnir, displacing ordinary destiny until birth.
- **Terrain hex tile:** a large modular landscape unit containing many content opportunities; distinct from world content nodes and Destiny Knots.
- **Focus:** deliberate hold-left-click attention that slows the character and opens executable contextual actions.
- **Priority:** player-selected task/quest thread whose current Need guides Divine Orientation.
- **Stonebound:** long-term deep-earth loss of individuality in lore, potentially reversible while will remains; not ordinary sleep or automatic death.
- **Item provenance:** learned information about making/possession, propagated by Shards and actual exchange.
- **Temporary well-being state:** possible Well Fed/vitality buff; exact representation remains open and is not assumed to be a permanent acquired Trait.

## Terms refined through answer 202

- **Common task slot:** default stone-board slot prioritizing oldest personal Need; community fallback if none remain.
- **Crest board:** 4 → 8 → 16 → 8 → 4 → 1 quest-slot layout, ending at a Knot and continuing into another Crest.
- **State:** active temporary condition; all states act mechanically while the oldest is visually represented.
- **Outing use:** a village buff duration unit consumed immediately on returning to the settlement.
- **Creator:** one of the two sources of a child's inherited foundation; communal final shaping and upbringing do not create a permanent exclusive parent role.
- **Repair:** independent Trait/tools/materials capability restoring a Broken item to its previous Okay condition.
- **World freeze:** no simulation progress without an actively simulating peer; saved reference state may still exist on storage nodes.
