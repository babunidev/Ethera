# Identity, Traits, Preferences and Needs

**Current concept through answer 251 · updated 2026-09-13.**

The filename retains the former word Desires so existing links keep working. Desires are no longer a separate character system.

## Character System

Playable people belong to the persistent world. They keep identity, knowledge, objects, life stage, relationships, tasks and condition across AI and player inhabitation. They are not resettable account-owned avatars. AI sustains ordinary community life; players advance Destiny and initiate reproduction.

Traits express abilities, appearance, personality and persistent preferences. Needs express current requirements. Shards carry learned functional information. Items, bodily states and relationships complete the interaction context. Strength should visibly affect form; traits should be embodied rather than merely displayed as statistics.

## Traits

Traits are binary, without ranks or stacking: Kind + Kind = Kind. Acquired personal Traits remain; temporary injury or loss of a tool restricts an action without erasing permanent competence. Equipment capabilities are conditional on the item. World-entity Traits and states can change and are not subject to an indiscriminate permanence rule.

All Traits use a shared flat matching vocabulary. Categories help authors manage content pools, but the interaction evaluator compares exact requirements. Foundational identities such as Grimnir, Humanoid, Earth Elemental and Individual can also be Traits. Separate spawn/reward pools prevent accidental creature-only abilities on playable people. Foundational Traits are not specified as consuming the 27 personal birth slots.

Examples include Strong, Funny, Loved, Fast, Good Climber, Good Speaker, Green Thumb, Knows Herbs, Knows Language, Knows Element and Knows Magic. These examples are not a final catalogue. Learned broad expertise is a Trait; a bear's reported location is a Shard.

## Desires

This heading is retained for old links. Persistent attractions now live as preference Traits: Likes Strong, Likes Kind, Likes Sacred. A person may have both Strong and Likes Strong. Absence of Likes Strong is neutral; a negative Dislikes system was considered and not selected. Temporary wants are Needs.

Matching remains exact: actor Likes Kind + target Kind can enable an authored greeting/Recognition interaction. Preference Traits can concern people, objects or places. More item qualities give more possible matches, not universal superiority. No separate 27-Desire array or inherited/birth/youth Desire quota remains an active rule. How preference Traits are distributed within the retained birth and youth model is not yet decided.

## Needs

Needs are structured objects with ownership, fulfillment requirements, task linkage and author-set importance. They support alternatives and usually search for matching solutions; special Needs can name an exact item or entity. They may arise from injury, repair, ordinary life or quests.

A Need may exist before a solution has been created. When the player exchanges knowledge at the fountain, resolve an existing free valid solution or create one at an appropriate random loot-capable world location. Provide the information through a Shard. Natural discovery, existing supplies, crafting and assistance can solve a Need without this consultation. See [Need and task lifecycle](../06_Gameplay/Needs_Tasks_and_Solution_Lifecycle.md) for the complete current rule.

Each Need completes independently. Consumptive requirements require sufficient separate supply. Prevent circular prerequisites and manufactured preserve/destroy demands on the same unique object. Solvability is a design guarantee about attainable routes, not guaranteed player success or immunity from final dissolution at Atranor.

## Trait Generation

The birth foundation remains 27 latent personal Traits: 9 from each creator and 9 from the allowed personal pool, without enforced Combat/Trickery/Aid/Social balance. Distinct-draw handling at birth is still open. The new no-duplicate rule for Destiny rewards does not silently settle birth draws.

Playable youth reveals latent qualities through participation; a final ritual establishes adulthood, first role recognition and Destiny. The former separate youth Desire development track is removed. Earlier local unmatched-preference pressure can inform future content as a design direction, but its weighting and integration into the unified Trait pool need clarification; existing people are not rewritten to supply a match.

Current learned-Trait progression is random within the completed Destiny branch's pool, one new Trait for each branch finished all the way into the Knot. Do not grant a Trait for every intermediate action or quest. Avoid already-owned Traits; an exhausted pool yields a random non-Trait fallback. Later teachers and discoveries are deferred. See [Destiny rewards](../06_Gameplay/Destiny_Quests_and_Progression.md#rewards).

Crafting qualities remain independent and can contribute together deterministically. Broadening Traits adds possibilities rather than numeric ranks.

## Exact matching, duplicate Traits, and independent Needs

preference Trait–Trait and Need–Shard matches are exact. Related qualities do not automatically substitute for the required counterpart. Finding Kind when one preferences Kind should be significant; the design does not broaden it into any vaguely helpful quality.

Traits are presence, not stacks: **Kind + Kind = Kind**. Duplicate sources do not improve a quality, rank it up, or unlock another interaction. An item remains a distinct physical object with its own traits, but character compatibility checks only require presence. Source tracking when temporary item effects end still needs specification. Duplicate draw handling does not yet establish 27 distinct effective traits at birth.

Each Need has its own completion. If two Needs concern the same person, place, Shard, or object, resolving one does not automatically resolve both. Repeated consumable requirements require sufficient separate supply; no accidental double-spending of the same meat or item. Tasks usually group Needs, while basic social, cultural, food, and rest Needs can exist outside quests.

Source: [answer 134](../../Morkahja-Concept-Sources.md#turn-771a5841-da17-4a04-8962-a1f8e604fb52), [answer 135](../../Morkahja-Concept-Sources.md#turn-9615656b-8fa1-43aa-83ce-0fdeb9ad8c58), [answer 136](../../Morkahja-Concept-Sources.md#turn-fb75fc58-50fa-4451-9d66-e01e89498843), [answer 137](../../Morkahja-Concept-Sources.md#turn-524f5a34-8d68-471d-94ca-a08337749441), [answer 147](../../Morkahja-Concept-Sources.md#turn-bbb21991-6ffc-4722-86c5-a8f5f8c87246).



## Guaranteed fulfillment without manufactured conflict

Each actively resolved Need must have a fitting attainable fulfillment. Fountain consultation now triggers the search/generation check; Need creation alone does not. Do not manufacture mutually exclusive demands on the same unique object: an intact-item Need and a break-item Need receive different suitable instances, including an already broken version or another breakable object.

Dependency chains may expand while active, but must not return to their own unresolved requirements. If A requires B, B requires C, and C would require A, choose a different fulfillment for C. If A and C both require meat, provide enough separate portions. The challenge is the journey and skillful interactions, not an impossible generation puzzle. Existing world content can still be reused where these guarantees hold; this is not permission to erase causal item history.

Source: [answer 139](../../Morkahja-Concept-Sources.md#turn-bbb21aa5-8457-4739-bea1-0d29a88d83ea), [answer 147](../../Morkahja-Concept-Sources.md#turn-bbb21991-6ffc-4722-86c5-a8f5f8c87246).


## Unified preferences and descriptive identity

Persistent preferences are Traits; current wants are Needs. Exact matching is unchanged, while the extra Desire data layer is removed. Role is derived flair and contributes no separate ability or generation weight. The old Desire counts are superseded rather than added on top of the unified Trait pool.

Source: [answer 238](../../Morkahja-Concept-Sources.md#turn-c2b28c15-4156-44bf-8f67-b593ec19c209), [answer 239](../../Morkahja-Concept-Sources.md#turn-41325c0c-dcc4-4b52-9660-a1d54eaef606), [answer 240](../../Morkahja-Concept-Sources.md#turn-21206fec-5c51-455d-9925-95cc0669ba29).
