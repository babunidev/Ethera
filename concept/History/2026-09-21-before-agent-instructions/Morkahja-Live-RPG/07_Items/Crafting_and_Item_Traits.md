# Crafting and Item Traits

Crafting transforms physical materials through tools, capability and a performed interaction. The relevant inputs and expertise determine the qualities of the result.

## Predictable outcomes

The same relevant person, materials, tools and action produce a reliable result. Learning how a process works gives the player dependable knowledge to use again.

Materials and tools carry Traits. The crafter's applicable qualities and knowledge determine which contributions reach the finished object.

## Independent expertise

Crafter, Leatherworker and Cloak Crafter are independent Traits. A person with all three contributes each relevant capability when making a leather cloak. A person with one uses the contribution that one supplies.

## Quality and compatibility

Soft, Warm, Durable and Fine illustrate qualities that a crafted object can carry. Each opens potential matches with preferences or Needs.

A Soft cloak matters to a person seeking softness. Another recipient might need Warm. A richer set of qualities gives the same object more possible uses and recipients.

Clothing requires materials, labor and care. Its availability reflects actual production in the community.

## Basic crafting and further knowledge

Traits, tools, materials and a suitable crafting place enable a basic craft. Relevant knowledge can enrich the result. A temporary crafting task can support the decision to seek that knowledge before making the object.

A person might make a simple spear immediately, or consult the cultural source to learn more about its material or technique. Knowledge, tool skill and material specialization can then contribute further item Traits.

## Making creates knowledge

The maker receives a Shard recording the creation of the object. Exchanging that knowledge or contributing it to the source lets others learn its provenance. Crafting adds both a physical object and information to communal life.

## Repair

Repair uses its own relevant capability, tools and materials. A repairer can restore an object made by someone else. Successful repair restores the previous condition and qualities completely.

Broken equipment produces a repair Need. The player can prioritize it and follow its requirements toward the necessary capability and supplies.

## Grimnir expression

Stone shaping, metalwork, nourishing mineral preparation and crystal resonance connect craft to Grimnir life. Ferrumis expresses refinement through repeated care and effort. A recognizable maker's method can appear in a shaped surface, joined component or finished tool.

The Spiral Forge-Ring and geometric material work carry cultural meaning. Later Grimnir works extend this craft into anchor crystals, sky-core minerals and floating architecture.

## Shaping a club in place

[Shape Weapon](../06_Gameplay/Shape_Weapon.md) acts directly on a world object carrying Rock and Shapeable. Weapon Shaping and a healthy usable arm supply the actor requirements. The target is already the suitable source for one primitive club. Success consumes its current manifestation and equips the created Weapon / Stone Weapon in Small Weapon, replacing the old item onto the ground when occupied.
