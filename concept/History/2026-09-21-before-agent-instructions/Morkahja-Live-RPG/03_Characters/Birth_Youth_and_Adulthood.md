# Birth, Youth and Adulthood

A life develops through shared creation, community support and participation. Each stage brings a different relationship to the world and to player inhabitation.

## Shared creation

Two willing Grimnir with compatible Traits and Trust initiate the joining interaction. They contribute fragments of geomantic identity to a third individual forming outside their bodies. Their Reproduction Destinies temporarily take the place of ordinary Destiny.

A mineral-rich resonance bath provides the growing body's material setting. Stone supplies substance, water carries dissolved minerals, warmth supports transformation, and air and surface exposure maintain balance. A small crystalline growth gathers material while its awareness and identity develop.

Early communication can be a faint vibration, a response to touch or a returned rhythm. Care involves attending to what the emerging individual needs.

## Reproduction Destinies

Each creator receives a quest tree. Their trees can overlap while containing different tasks: gathering food and minerals, retrieving knowledge or Essence from a sacred place, preparing the nest, processing supplies and performing paired rituals or nourishing resonance.

Some requirements involve both creators; others send them on separate journeys. A stable nesting place with prepared stone and resonant crystals sustains the developing child while they travel. They return to tend it and complete further interactions. Community assistance contributes to progress.

Existing suitable supplies count. A well-prepared settlement can support rapid creation; occupied resources or simultaneous undertakings can lead to longer gathering journeys. Progress follows completed requirements and care.

## Birth and inheritance

Final shaping involves several community members. The new life joins the settlement as a child of the community, with the two initiators retained as the sources of its inherited identity.

The birth foundation contains 27 latent Traits: nine from each creator and nine from the personal pool. These qualities shape the individual as they emerge. Communal care supports that identity throughout childhood.

At birth, the Reproduction Destiny ends and the child enters autonomous village life. Independent form and awareness distinguish the new individual from the developing presence in the bath.

## Early childhood

Young children move through settlement life under simulation. The community cares for them and develops the support necessary for meaningful participation.

Playable youth begins when the settlement can support the person. Housing and nourishment are part of that support. Suitable existing homes and surplus provision count immediately; otherwise the child continues in communal care until the conditions are met.

## Playable youth

A youth has the elemental baseline and some expressed personal Traits. Helping, exploring, learning, meeting people and completing tasks reveal further qualities. Latent Traits stay concealed until they emerge through participation.

Village life itself provides development. A youth may contribute materials, join a social interaction, investigate a landmark and help with a communal task. Each activity develops their ability to take part in the world.

## The adulthood rite

When youth development is complete, a final coming-of-age quest prepares a cultural ritual. Materials, actions and the person's emerging capabilities can give the rite its form.

The ritual establishes adulthood, reveals the first role and begins Destiny. All birth Traits are now expressed. The role describes the dominant pattern among those qualities; the available branches lead toward a purpose revealed through further play.

## Continuing life

Adults gain further Traits by finishing Destiny branches and contribute through their actual abilities. Their role title can change as their dominant qualities change, with renewed recognition at the fountain.

Players initiate reproduction and drive Destiny. Capable AI contributes to ordinary care and cooperative work while the settlement runs.
