# Roles and Baseline Abilities

## Roles express a person

A role is a cultural title drawn from dominant current Traits. The adulthood rite reveals the first role. Later fountain visits update the community's recognition as the person's qualities develop.

A hunter tracks through their actual capabilities; a craftsperson works through relevant craft Traits, tools and knowledge. Tasks consider the whole person, including capabilities outside their dominant calling.

The cultures give roles distinct meanings. Grimnir traditions include forging, architecture, navigation and memorykeeping. Rokur emphasize smithing and communal work; Verdani include healing, waterkeeping and gardening; Aevis cultivate scholarship, architecture and crystal craft.

## Embodied foundations

Humanoid bodies share ordinary physical actions. Elemental nature supplies further capacities, available by playable youth. Personal Traits, tools, equipment and current condition shape how these foundations can be expressed.

A usable arm supports a strike. A secure grip helps climbing. An injured limb can prevent its dependent actions while the person's permanent qualities remain. A carried item contributes its required properties while the person supplies the appropriate Trait or knowledge for using it.

## Perception and craft

A Grimnir touching a stone shelf can feel nearby footsteps or a strained support. Handling a mineral gives a bodily sense of its suitability. They can smooth an edge, bind grains or shape a small hollow through patient work.

Larger structures, fine mechanisms and specialized techniques draw on further capabilities. Reading a distant tunnel, opening a lock, shaping a weapon and constructing a home each use the appropriate authored interaction and requirements.

## Independent expertise

Crafter, Leatherworker and Cloak Crafter are separate Traits. When all apply to an action, their contributions combine. Equipment adds its own conditional possibilities, while current injuries and states shape availability.

The [first twelve interactions](../06_Gameplay/First_Twelve_Grimnir_Interactions.md) give these elemental foundations concrete functions across Combat, Trickery, Aid and Social.

## Authored shaping and memory actions

The elemental capacity Earthshaping expresses the Grimnir relationship with stone. **Shape Weapon** is the concrete world-object action, enabled by the **Weapon Shaping** Trait and its other requirements. **Echo Attunement** retrieves needed knowledge; **Echo Imprint**, supported by Echo Imprint Trait, contributes knowledge to an Echo-bearing target.
