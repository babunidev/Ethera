# Art, Audio, Camera and Movement

Morkahja has a restrained, serious fantasy atmosphere: ancient, strange and quietly dreamlike. Stylization leaves room for imagination, while small movements, sounds and changes in light reward attention.

## Visual direction

The first Grimnir community is discovering individual and shared life among stone and wilderness. Broad grassland, savanna and hills create space around modest shelters, work areas and sacred places.

Grimnir bodies have layered mineral plates, luminous ore and crystal seams, and gemstone eyes. Continuous shaped stone and resonant material give their surroundings a related visual language. Later Skylands extend it into curved bridges, leaning forms and crystal lattices scattering light.

Magic can appear as subtle distortion or energy flowing into and out of terrain and living things. The world remains calm enough for these details to be noticed.

## People and possessions

Bodies communicate condition through posture, movement, brightness, solidity and definition. Well-being can make a person more vivid; weakening can dim or blur their form.

Equipment, bags, bundles and clothes change the silhouette. The oldest active visible state leads the animation, while every active state continues to affect mechanics and appears in the character panel.

## Sound and communication

Stone contact, shaping sounds, low hums, tool rhythms and shared resonance give Grimnir life its sound. Music, gatherings and cultural stories bring communal activity into the environment.

Players communicate through contextual actions, gestures, movement and knowledge exchange. Player text and voice chat are absent. Authored character sound and environmental storytelling belong to the world's own expression.

## Movement

Right click sets a destination. Movement is direct, precise and physical. Ordinary movement has one base walking pace, gradual acceleration at departure and deceleration before stopping. Small natural variations can give motion life. Situation and buffs can alter pace, including more urgent combat movement. The player chooses the destination; the situation governs tempo.

Selecting an interaction sends the character to the authored position beside the target and turns them toward it before the skill phase. Combat engagement maintains natural orientation and positioning; disengagement returns independent movement.

## Camera

An elevated angled view keeps the person and surrounding details visible. Left-click and drag on the inhabited character to orbit the camera; the mouse wheel zooms from close observation to a broad view. The player can look back along a route and retain that view as they move.

The camera follows the character's position and preserves the player's chosen angle and zoom throughout movement and encounters. Spectators choose an existing on-screen person to follow.

## The Memory Fountain

A broad stone basin gathers people around an irregular rise of dark stone, pale crystal and metal veins. Lower surfaces are rounded and polished by touch. Higher crystalline ridges branch upward; fine light travels along them, spreads across their tips and fades toward the base.

Shallow mineral-rich water circulates around the base, catches sunlight and reveals small resonant ripples. Knowledge resides in the stone and crystal. Water conveys vibration and connects the Fountain's material language to nursery baths and healing places.

Charcoal stone, cloudy quartz and warm mineral seams carry occasional green or amber inclusions. A contribution briefly brightens particular veins. A layered low hum, occasional crystal notes and water in narrow channels give it a quiet presence. Grimnir feel the Fountain through their feet before touching it.

## First models and symbolic challenges

Khar-Morr's first model uses the selected bare low-poly design: a powerful female Grimnir figure composed of angular mineral masses. Blender builds the character and the supplied Fountain design. Movement and interaction animation develop incrementally.

Skill graphics use simple straight-edged symbols resembling rough gold or metal plate. Positioning and timing remain visible in the shape itself. [Player-Skill Phases](../06_Gameplay/Player_Skill_Phases.md) describes Shape Trace and Shard Fit.

## The first village center

The [environment brief](Milestone_1_Environment.md) defines continuous stone forms around the separate Fountain, a level gathering area within varied bedrock, converging footpaths, integrated seats, sparse grass and moss, and warm daylight. Khar-Morr begins almost entirely as bare living stone. Her reference views and the Fountain's multi-view sheet guide Blender modeling.
