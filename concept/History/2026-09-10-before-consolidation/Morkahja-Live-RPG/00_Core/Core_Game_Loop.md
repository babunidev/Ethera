# Core Game Loop

**Status:** Consolidated through interview answer 104, 2026-09-10. Later decisions take precedence; open details remain explicit.

## A session

Choose an available life and accept its role → orient in the ongoing situation → move and observe → notice signs and details → perform contextual actions → discover, cooperate, craft, or pursue an objective → return with physical goods and knowledge → leave the character to continuing world life.

The moment-to-moment rhythm is **move → observe → notice → interpret → interact → continue**. Quiet discovery matters more than speed or collecting large amounts of loot.

## Purpose and tasks

Accepted tasks and active Needs receive subtle Divine Orientation. The knowledge fountain supplies relevant knowledge and suitable simulation-generated community work through direct cultural interactions. The panel explains the character and discovered actions; it is not a task-selection dashboard.

Failure creates recoverable detours and new Needs with possible solutions. Players can follow other available destiny approaches through play, without permanently losing branch options.

## The generational cycle

World → community → birth → simulated early childhood → playable youth and participation → coming-of-age ritual → role and destiny → expanding and converging adventures → persistent objects, knowledge, and communal conditions for future people.

Only players advance destiny; AI maintains ordinary life. The first playable focus is one Grimnir settlement. Population contributes playable slots once individuals reach the playable phase.
