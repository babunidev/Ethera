# Needs

**Status:** Consolidated through interview answer 104, 2026-09-10. Later decisions take precedence; open details remain explicit.

## Situational requirements

Needs arise from quests, side quests, community tasks, exchanges, and changed circumstances. Persistent Desires seek Traits; situational Needs connect with Shards and actionable solutions.

## A solution must exist

When a Need is created, the system checks for a possible solution. If it exists, connect to it; otherwise generate what makes resolution possible. This does not guarantee immediate success or reveal every step to the player.

Failure can create a treatment Need, a replacement-tool Need, a search for an escaped creature, or another recovery requirement. Failure changes the route without permanently closing it.

An exchange may require meeting another person's Need or Desire first. The resulting chain has no fixed depth cap and can be worked back through after a match is found.

## Acting on a Need

The character panel explains Needs and relevant Shards. It is informational rather than a menu for operating the character. Accepting a task or performing another appropriate in-world action establishes the current activity. The player can pursue another available branch through play, without a recurring content-choice menu.

The knowledge fountain supplies suitable community work and relevant knowledge through direct cultural interactions. Divine Orientation guides toward the next useful step, even when current reality changes independently of personal knowledge.

Whether guidance also responds to broad destiny with no active Need/task is the latest unanswered question.
