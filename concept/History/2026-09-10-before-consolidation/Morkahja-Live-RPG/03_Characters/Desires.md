# Desires

**Status:** Consolidated through interview answer 104, 2026-09-10. Later decisions take precedence; open details remain explicit.

Desires are persistent directions paired with Traits in people or the wider world. A person may desire a quality they already possess. The counterpart can be on a creature, object, garment, tool, or place, as well as another character.

## Origin and expression

The target remains 27 Desires: 9 inherited in total, 9 generated at birth, and 9 formed through experience. Exact parental selection remains open. Latent desires stay hidden until they emerge. Playable youth reveals or develops them through tasks and ordinary participation, completing youth development before the final adulthood rite.

## Generation pressure

The design vocabulary gives every Desire a matching Trait, but a matching instance need not exist immediately. If suitable world content can answer an unmet Desire, generate or prioritize it. If the counterpart requires a person, prioritize that trait in future newborn generation rather than rewriting an existing person.

This influence is local to the settlement for now. It does not manipulate distant cultures. Desires can remain unmatched while the world develops toward a possible counterpart.

This is a refinement of completely unconstrained random generation: the 27-trait structure and absence of category balancing remain, but future traits can have local desire-based priority. Exact weighting and which draw it affects are open.

## Play

Desires shape social matches, destiny possibilities, and equipment compatibility. More item traits create more potential matches, not universal superiority. Compatibility primarily opens interactions rather than changing hidden success rolls.
