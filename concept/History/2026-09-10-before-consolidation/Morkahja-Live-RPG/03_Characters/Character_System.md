# Character System

**Status:** Established concept, updated through answer 104 on 2026-09-10; later user corrections take precedence.

## World-owned lives

Every playable character already belongs to the persistent world and community. They alternate between AI and player control. A later player continues that person's actual history, rather than resetting an avatar.

## Identity

Traits provide the building blocks for abilities, personality, appearance, and eventually social role and destiny. Desires add persistent direction; Needs and Shards provide situational requirements and knowledge. Strength should visibly affect form; the person should look and act like the qualities that compose them. Family and social connections may provide a starting network, although relationship detail is not settled.

Characters also carry current knowledge, objects, life stage, ongoing objectives, and embodied well-being. Knowledge shards provide situational information and should not be confused with permanently acquired traits.

## Life course

Birth creates 27 latent traits. Early childhood is simulated. Playable youth reveals hidden qualities through participation; a final ritual reveals role and begins adulthood and destiny. Players advance destiny, while AI maintains ordinary life. Exhaustion of godly essence leads to Atranor and possible continuation or dissolution.

See [Traits](Traits.md), [Childhood](Childhood.md), and [Adulthood](Adulthood.md).


## Update — 2026-09-10

The character now has four explicit conceptual sets: Traits, Desires, Needs, and Shards. There are 27 target Desires with inherited, birth-generated, and experiential origins. Universal humanoid actions and elemental abilities provide a baseline modified by personal qualities, role, equipment, and injuries. See [Desires](Desires.md), [Needs](Needs.md), and [Baseline Actions](Baseline_Actions.md).
