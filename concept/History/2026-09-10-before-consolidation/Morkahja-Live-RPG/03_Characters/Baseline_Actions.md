# Baseline Actions

**Status:** Consolidated through interview answer 104, 2026-09-10. Later decisions take precedence; open details remain explicit.

Characters have ordinary embodied actions without requiring a personal trait for every attempt. Broadly humanoid bodies share a common foundation, with different elemental abilities layered on top.

## Availability

All elemental baseline abilities are available when the person becomes playable in youth. Early childhood is simulated rather than player-controlled. This resolves the previous question about waiting for adulthood to unlock the baseline.

Shared humanoid baseline → elemental baseline → personal traits and desires → role → equipment → current state/injuries → usable contextual actions.

Baseline embodiment can be represented as underlying traits, but no rule deducts it from the 27 personal birth traits. Personal qualities expand possibilities; injury can restrict actions without removing permanent traits.

## Equipment and execution

A character can physically use or wear equipment without a matching build. Compatibility unlocks more of its interaction potential. Equipment-granted capabilities are conditional on the equipment and do not automatically become permanent birth/acquired traits.

Character qualities determine available interactions; player skill determines their execution. Tests vary by interaction rather than always requiring timing.

Exact basic action lists, elemental abilities, physical fitting rules, and input bindings remain open.
