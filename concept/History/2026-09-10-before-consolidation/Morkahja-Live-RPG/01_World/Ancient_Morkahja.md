# Ancient Morkahja

**Status:** Established concept, consolidated from the project conversation; later user corrections take precedence.

## Era

The game inhabits a very early age of the existing Morkahja world: primal societies, possibly before agriculture, but already living in settlements. The four elemental peoples have not yet encountered one another. The broader concept includes each people, but the first playable version focuses on one Grimnir settlement before expanding.

Small houses, wooden fences or palisades, natural shrines, altars, and stone circles occupy a landscape dominated by wilderness. Prairie and savanna are initial visual impressions, not a restriction to one biome. Rituals may involve animals or other sacred gifts.

## Broader world reference

The supplied world introduction describes a primal world shaped by elemental gods, with living magic, frozen mountain ranges, desert sanctuaries, drifting cities, extraordinary beauty, and the danger of corrupted magic. The four peoples' elemental natures shape their homelands and everyday life.

The source's developed cities and cultural institutions are broader lore references. Their exact early forms in this game's ancient era have not been fixed. Do not automatically populate the starting villages with every later city's institution or technology.

## Still open

The chronology of agriculture, elemental creation, and the arrival or awakening of magic needs reconciliation with the larger world lore. No new historical timeline is invented here.
