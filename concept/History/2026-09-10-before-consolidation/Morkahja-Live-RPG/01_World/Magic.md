# Magic

**Status:** Established concept, consolidated from the project conversation; later user corrections take precedence.

## Character and atmosphere

Magic is powerful, rare, primal, and mysterious. It is not a routine display of conventional spellcasting mages. Shamans, druids, ritualists, stone circles, sacred places, visions, and natural altars fit the intended period.

The game occurs before, or at the beginning of, magic arriving or awakening in the world. Visible energy can pour through terrain and living things, soaking into and flowing out of matter. Subtle distortion and motion make reality feel charged and strange.

## Purpose and integrity

Following destiny reinforces individuality and can sustain or replenish energy. Purposeful participation in community also keeps a being healthy. Aimless wandering eventually brings fatigue and a need to return home.

Weakening integrity can lead to losing the boundary between an individual and their element. Excessive connection to a particular corrupt magic can instead transform a being into something sickening and hostile to the world.

These conditions appear through the body and environment rather than a morality meter or explicit warning popup. See [Diegetic Interface](../07_Presentation/Diegetic_Interface.md) and [Death and Dissolution](../03_Characters/Death_and_Dissolution.md).

## Unresolved rules

Spell lists, costs, corruption thresholds, and the relationship between elemental bodies and magic's historical arrival are not specified.


## Divine Orientation

The gods' view of the world supports current directional guidance separately from mortal knowledge. Signs are subtle and intermittent; they guide the next step without deciding success. One unified system comes first; god-specific styles are deferred. Atranor remains the established name despite later speech-transcription variants. See [Environmental Guidance](../07_Presentation/Environmental_Guidance.md).
