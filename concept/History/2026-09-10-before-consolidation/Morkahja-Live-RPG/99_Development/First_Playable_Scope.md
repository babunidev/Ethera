# First Playable Scope

**Status:** Consolidated through interview answer 104, 2026-09-10. Later decisions take precedence; open details remain explicit.

## One Grimnir settlement first

Build one functioning settlement and one culture before expanding to the other peoples. The chosen starting culture is earth-aligned Grimnir. This replaces the assumption that four settlements must ship together.

The other peoples remain part of Morkahja's broader lore and longer-term design. They have not yet met in this ancient era. Local generation priorities do not reach other cultures for now.

## What the experience should prove

A living autonomous community; simulated early childhood and playable youth; participation-driven development; a coming-of-age ritual revealing role and beginning destiny; cultural knowledge and useful tasks; a procedural local world; meaningful interactions and persistent objects.

This is a concept focus, not an approved implementation milestone checklist or a promise to ship every system at once.

## Emotional direction

Mystery, wonder, curiosity, and a dreamlike low-stimulation sense of a great civilization just beginning. Grimnir are emerging from mindless stone or earth existence into individuals and community. The beginning feels significant without loud spectacle.

Detailed architecture, roles, food needs, reproduction, and cultural content are deferred. Hunter examples do not establish that Grimnir hunt or eat like humans.

One unified Divine Orientation system comes first. Identifying individual gods through distinct guidance styles is a later idea. Starting population size is not decided.
