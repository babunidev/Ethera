# Ideas

**Status:** Established concept, updated through answer 104 on 2026-09-10; later user corrections take precedence.

## Retained optional ideas

- A very lightweight account diary or timeline of notable participation. It must not provide permanent gameplay power or ownership.
- Nine initially active traits in childhood, revealed randomly from the latent pool. The growth rule is established; the starting number was phrased tentatively.
- In-world lore inspection while queued, including buildings and character information. Detailed visibility rules remain open under follow-only spectating.
- An early notion of notifying players when too few people are active and the village suffers. This was not finalized and should be considered alongside competent offline AI and low-pressure sessions.

## Illustrative content from the conversation

- A shared bear adventure with different characters seeking claw, heart, or meat.
- A bear site later hosting a flower or lost family item after the old contents are gone.
- A cave gaining a new occupant after its earlier purpose ends.
- Finding something glittering in a tree that a character with climbing ability can investigate.
- A distant mountain destiny whose route contains intermediate encounters.
- Cultural signs such as a rising bird, directed light, or Grimnir stones pulling toward a destination.

These examples explain systems; they do not establish a mandatory starter quest sequence.

## Superseded explorations

Proximity voice chat, free-flight spectating, personal account progression, and destiny/social-role activation at birth were replaced by later decisions. Do not implement the early versions merely because they remain in the source transcript.


## Update — 2026-09-10

## Superseded today

- Memory burden, per-shard weight, fixed capacity, and oldest-first eviction are removed. The brief capacity proposal was reversed later the same day.
- Mandatory offloading to lighten oneself is no longer a mechanic.
- “Longings” and persistent “Needs” are now named Desires.
- Automatic knowledge synchronization merely by proximity is not used; a cultural interaction must be executed.
- A node-local history is unnecessary; collective Shards hold historical layers.
- Detailed medical wounds are deferred in favor of broad functional injuries.
- Negative social reputation and hatred are outside initial scope.
- Full dependency-web UI, guidance precision tiers, and a separate priority-emotion layer are not needed.

The earlier account diary remains optional and separate from functional Shards. Unique interactions for every specific pairing remain an eventual aspiration, with a smaller culturally appropriate library first.


## Later ideas from answers 60–104

Distinct gods may eventually have recognizable styles of guidance, but the initial system is unified and the pantheon partly mysterious. A slight directional swiftness and random sign intervals were examples, not fixed mechanics.

Further cultures follow the first Grimnir settlement. Detailed Grimnir content is deferred. Examples of hunters, winter preparation, cloak qualities, and branch counts explain systems rather than committing specific content.

Superseded within this interval: hierarchical crafting specializations, hidden equipment-success percentages, and an operational task/priority menu. Early-childhood play and time-based adulthood are also replaced. The earlier mention of a full destiny tree resolves to hidden future nodes and later informational-only UI; do not build a full quest graph from that phrase.
