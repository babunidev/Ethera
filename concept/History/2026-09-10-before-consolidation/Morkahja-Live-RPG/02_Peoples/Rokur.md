# Rokur

**Status:** Established lore from the supplied world introduction.

## The Embersworn
Towering beings of living flame, the Rokur inhabit the northern Fyrskar Mountains, where glaciers surround volcanic peaks. The cold helps keep their fiery bodies balanced.

## Homeland and settlements
Cities are carved into mountainsides. Great communal hearths warm vast stone halls.

## Community and everyday life
Life revolves around clans, craftsmanship, shared responsibility, and the discipline needed to contain their own strength. Smithing, training, and communal work fill their days. Evenings bring shared meals and stories around the hearth.

## Knowledge traditions from the RPG conversation

Clan-Hearths, Hearthkeepers, storytellers, and oral teaching preserve knowledge and support local reports.

## Place in this game

The ancient setting keeps the peoples separate before they have met. The first playable version begins with one Grimnir community; the others are later expansion scope. The developed settlements described above come from the broader world introduction; their precise early forms remain open. Roles are culture-specific callings assigned at adulthood from traits. Their exact roster has not been chosen.

All peoples participate in the shared character system: 27 latent birth traits, playable youth, adult roles and destinies, temporary player inhabitation, and continuing AI life. How reproduction and dissolution appear for each elemental body still needs detailed adaptation.
