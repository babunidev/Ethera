# Grimnir

**Status:** Established lore from the supplied world introduction.

## The Stonewrought
Formed from stone, metal, and crystal, the Grimnir inhabit the Skylands: vast floating islands shaped into cities that drift above the world.

## Homeland and settlements
Airborne cities are sustained by their communities' work. When Skylands gather, bridges connect them for trade, reunions, and great communal celebrations.

## Community and everyday life
Communities are organized around crafts. Forgers, architects, navigators, and memorykeepers each contribute. Creation is central to life, and an artisan's work can shape the character of an entire city.

## Knowledge traditions from the RPG conversation

Memory stones, architectural records, Memory-Keepers, and traveling Skykeepers preserve and carry knowledge through craft and living memory.

## Place in this game

The ancient setting keeps the peoples separate before they have met. The first playable version begins with one Grimnir community; the others are later expansion scope. The developed settlements described above come from the broader world introduction; their precise early forms remain open. Roles are culture-specific callings assigned at adulthood from traits. Their exact roster has not been chosen.

All peoples participate in the shared character system: 27 latent birth traits, playable youth, adult roles and destinies, temporary player inhabitation, and continuing AI life. How reproduction and dissolution appear for each elemental body still needs detailed adaptation.

See [Cultural Knowledge Systems](../04_Community/Cultural_Knowledge_Systems.md), [Birth and Inheritance](../03_Characters/Birth_and_Inheritance.md), and [Ancient Morkahja](../01_World/Ancient_Morkahja.md).


## Update — 2026-09-10

All four peoples share a broadly humanoid baseline of ordinary actions, with different elemental abilities layered on top. Baseline embodiment may be represented as underlying traits. All elemental baseline abilities are present by playable youth; the specific action catalogue is still undefined. Knowledge exchanges and social bonding take place through nearby, culturally appropriate interactions. See [Baseline Actions](../03_Characters/Baseline_Actions.md).


## First playable culture

The initial playable focus is one Grimnir settlement. The desired feeling is individuality and community beginning to emerge from mindless stone, with mystery, wonder, and a quiet dreamlike tone. Specific early architecture, diet, roles, and customs remain deferred; later Skyland lore is not automatically the first-settlement design. See [First Playable Scope](../99_Development/First_Playable_Scope.md).
