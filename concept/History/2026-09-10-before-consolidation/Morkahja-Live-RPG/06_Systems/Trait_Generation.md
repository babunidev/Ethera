# Trait Generation

**Status:** Consolidated through interview answer 104, 2026-09-10. Later decisions take precedence; open details remain explicit.

## Personal traits

The 27 birth-trait structure remains: 9 drawn from each parent and 9 from the full pool. Do not impose a balanced Combat/Trickery/Aid/Social spread or generate a predetermined role first.

## Local desire priority

Completely unconstrained randomness is refined: an unmet Desire whose counterpart must be a person can prioritize the matching trait in future newborns. Existing people are not overwritten. Influence stays local to the settlement.

How priority affects the parental versus global draws, its weights, and duplicate/conflict handling remain open. The rule does not guarantee immediate fulfillment or rewrite the 9+9+9 counts.

## Expression and growth

Early childhood is simulated. Youth is playable with all elemental baseline abilities and some expressed personal qualities. Latent traits and desires are hidden until revealed through activity. Completing youth development and the final ritual leads to adulthood, role revelation, and destiny beginning.

Permanent personal traits accumulate through relevant quests; 27 is not a lifetime cap. Flat crafting traits may each be inherited, generated, or earned, and applicable ones contribute together.

Desires have a separate target of 9 inherited total, 9 birth-generated, and 9 experiential. Their parental split and precise acquisition rules remain open. Baseline and equipment-dependent capabilities are not specified as consuming personal birth slots.
