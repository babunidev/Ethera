# Knowledge Shards

**Status:** Established concept, updated 2026-09-10. Later user decisions supersede earlier brainstorms; unresolved details are marked.

## Functional knowledge

Shards are situational information used to enable action and interaction, not a general autobiography or diary. Examples include a bear sighting, a cave's useful contents, knowledge of who owns an item, or a relationship fact that enables further social interaction.

Traits and Desires are persistent; Shards and Needs are situational. The design defines **Need ↔ Shard** counterparts. A shard is not a permanent acquired trait.

## Collection and sharing

Characters collect encountered functional knowledge without a shard capacity, per-shard weight, burden system, or oldest-first eviction. Those intermediate proposals were rejected in favor of simplicity.

Person-to-person sharing duplicates knowledge. Visiting the cultural collective-memory hub enriches its knowledge; memory-management and lightening are no longer gameplay requirements. The final simplification was summarized in the conversation as contributing copies. The user's phrase “leave everything there” does not define a mandatory personal wipe, so none is added here.

## Stale personal knowledge

A character may carry outdated knowledge indefinitely until a relevant exchange occurs. Information is not globally synchronized with the world.

Meeting someone with a newer shard for the same node creates an exchange opportunity. The player performs a culturally appropriate interaction to receive it. Consultation at a collective knowledge hub can also provide an update.

After obtaining newer information, the old personal shard is discarded and replaced. This is replacement of obsolete information, not capacity-based forgetting or decay of a permanent trait.

## Collective history

The communal archive keeps older shards layered alongside new ones. It supplies the newest relevant known information for a current Need. Nodes retain only current state; old node knowledge persists in external shard copies.

Exact timestamps, version identifiers, equality of topics within one node, and conflicting reports are implementation questions. No shard-quality or guidance-strength tiers are required.

See [Memory Wells](Memory_Wells.md), [Needs](../03_Characters/Needs.md), and [Interaction System](Interaction_System.md).


## Knowledge and divine guidance are distinct

Divine Orientation can follow the world's current next step without waiting for personal Shards to update. It does not synchronize or replace those Shards; factual knowledge still comes through local observation and performed exchanges. The later conversational phrase “full to the brim” does not reintroduce a fixed shard capacity or weight system.
