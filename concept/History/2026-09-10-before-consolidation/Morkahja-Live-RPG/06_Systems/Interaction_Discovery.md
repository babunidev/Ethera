# Interaction Discovery

**Status:** Consolidated through interview answer 104, 2026-09-10. Later decisions take precedence; open details remain explicit.

## Discover through play

Interactions can become available when the character's qualities and the actual situation fit. Discovery comes through quests, exploration, equipment use, meeting people, combat, and communal work. It is not designed as a combination-testing laboratory.

Before discovery, an interaction has no preview, silhouette, remaining-count hint, or suggested combination. Players learn it exists when the situation reveals it.

## Permanent character record

After discovery, the interaction remains documented in the character panel. The panel explains which traits, role, equipment, relationship, or conditions enable it.

If temporarily unavailable, it stays visible and clearly identifies the missing ingredient or blocking condition. Losing equipment or receiving an injury changes usability, not the record of discovery. The record belongs to the persistent character rather than granting account-owned power.

## In play versus in the panel

In-world contextual inputs perform actions directly, with cultural or physical animation. The panel provides optional explanation and flavor, not a requirement to inspect formulas during play.

Specific bindings and the exact discovery trigger remain open: the conversation ties discovery to encountering the revealing circumstances but does not define a separate mandatory successful-use test.
