# Collective Memory

**Status:** Established concept, updated 2026-09-10. Later user decisions supersede earlier brainstorms; unresolved details are marked.

Collective memory preserves functional knowledge shared by the community, including older states of the world. It is local cultural knowledge, not telepathy or a globally synchronized database.

## History is carried by shards

A node only needs its current contents and state. Its earlier history survives in shards held by individuals and the collective archive. Repopulating a node clears its old node-associated shard-state, not external copies already preserved.

The archive layers older information beside newer information rather than deleting outdated shards. A cave might have records of a bear sighting, its death, vacancy, and later occupants.

## Current usefulness

For a quest-related Need, the hub supplies the newest relevant knowledge it has. Historical browsing is not a major intended activity in the initial design. The system can trace known events and item possession to redirect an objective toward its present answer.

An individual can still act on an old report until they receive a newer shard through an actual interaction. On personal update, obsolete information is replaced; in collective memory it remains historical.

## Contribution

Characters enrich the collective archive with encountered knowledge through culturally appropriate interactions. Capacity, memory weight, and mandatory unburdening have been removed from the design.
