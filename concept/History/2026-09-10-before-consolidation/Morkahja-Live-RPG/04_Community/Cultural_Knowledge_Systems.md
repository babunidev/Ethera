## Shared function

These traditions collect current knowledge alongside history. A response may indicate something available in stores, someone who owns it, a known place, a recent sighting, or a person with further information. Those answer types are illustrative, not a finalized menu.

Knowledge is local and fallible. Larger archives and traveling keepers can broaden what is available, but reports can age or be incomplete.

## Ancient-era treatment

The precise early forms of these institutions need reconciliation with isolated primitive villages. Consultation is conveyed through cultural and contextual interactions or rituals, not free-form player voice or text conversation.


## Update — 2026-09-10

The hub primarily updates knowledge relevant to a Need, rather than inviting broad archive browsing. It retains older layers but supplies the newest relevant known shard. Personal updates also occur through performed exchanges with nearby characters; merely meeting does not synchronize them. Copying and cultural contribution remain simple, without memory weight or capacity mechanics. See [Memory Wells](../06_Systems/Memory_Wells.md).


## Direct task and knowledge interactions

Knowledge fountains also provide suitable community tasks. Exchange Knowledge and Accept Task are separate contextual actions with cultural animation, not a required task menu. First implement one Grimnir knowledge system; other cultures remain future scope. Shards remain locally transmitted, while Divine Orientation is a separate divine view of current reality.
