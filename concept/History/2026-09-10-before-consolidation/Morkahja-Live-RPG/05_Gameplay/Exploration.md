# Exploration

**Status:** Consolidated through interview answer 104, 2026-09-10. Later decisions take precedence; open details remain explicit.

Exploration rewards attention. The world should seem calm and almost empty at first glance, with details revealed by movement, sound, observation, and relevant character traits. Avoid carpeting the landscape with visible enemies on spawn points.

A rabbit leaving cover, a bird, movement in bushes, a glint between stones, or a newly noticed object can invite investigation. A bear's appearance is a meaningful encounter with options to avoid, observe, or engage through available interactions.

## Character-specific discovery

Qualities such as Keen Eye or climbing capability open different observations and actions. Ordinary baseline abilities still exist. Undiscovered interactions have no advance hints; discovering them through ordinary play adds them to the person's documented interaction library.

## Direction without noise

Divine Orientation supplies subtle intermittent signs toward the next useful step. Players learn to read them; signs are not constant stimulation. Guidance can follow current reality while personal shards remain locally acquired and potentially stale.

The interesting part is the journey and what happens at the destination, not aimless searching for the existence of a required objective. The player may still investigate other things or pursue another available branch through play.

## Bringing things home

Useful knowledge enriches collective memory. Objects require physical carrying arrangements, making return choices and cooperation meaningful. This is not a loot-hoarding game.

Purposeful activity and communal return maintain well-being. AI may explore within endurance limits without advancing personal destiny.
