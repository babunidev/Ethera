# Camera and Movement

**Status:** Established concept, updated 2026-09-10; later user corrections take precedence.

The game is 3D with an elevated perspective currently favored over an over-the-shoulder camera. Dota 2 is a key control and perspective reference; Baldur's Gate 2 and Icewind Dale were also named as perspective references.

Movement should be direct, precise, physical, and responsive. Exact input method, camera angle, zoom, rotation, and traversal controls have not been finalized. The references express a direction rather than a complete control specification.

## Spectator camera

Queued spectators can select visible characters and follow their lives. No free-flying camera is allowed under the latest decision. The earlier free-flight suggestion is superseded.

This restricts observation to existing lives rather than giving a player an independent scouting camera. The exact information available while following, and rules for switching targets, remain open.


## Update — 2026-09-10

Combat refines movement: engaging locks onto the opponent and the game handles natural facing and most positioning. The player chooses timely available responses. Disengaging releases that lock for independent movement or escape. This is a combat-specific refinement of direct control; it does not change the follow-only spectator camera. See [Combat](../05_Gameplay/Combat.md).
