# Audio and Voice

**Status:** Established concept, consolidated from the project conversation; later user corrections take precedence.

## Final communication decision

There is no proximity voice chat and no free player text chat. The earlier voice-chat proposal was explicitly withdrawn because a real player's voice may not fit the different bodies, ages, cultures, and personalities they inhabit.

Players communicate through contextual actions, signals, gestures, movement, role-specific abilities, and cultural knowledge exchange. These interactions depend on actual traits, roles, relationships, and matching information rather than a shallow generic emote list.

## Sound in the world

Sound remains part of immersion. Music, communal gatherings, and cultural activities matter to social gameplay; the source lore also includes Verdani music and festivals and Rokur hearth storytelling.

The absence of player voice chat does not establish silence or prohibit character vocalization. Voice acting, nonverbal vocal sounds, musical direction, subtitles, and the presentation of storytelling have not been decided.
