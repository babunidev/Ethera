# Aevis

## The Windweavers
Beings of air and aether, the Aevis dwell beneath the mountains in Celesterra and other hidden subterranean cities. Uncontrolled winds can unravel their bodies, so their sheltered homeland is essential to survival.

## Homeland and settlements
Luminous crystals form artificial skies above slender towers. Carefully guided currents carry movement and life through enormous caverns.

## Community and everyday life
Society values learning, mentorship, and disciplined magical practice. Daily life centers on scholarship, architecture, crystal craftsmanship, and maintaining the atmosphere that holds their world together.

## Knowledge traditions

Crystal archives, harmonic chambers, and Resonance Archives preserve knowledge through resonance and support communal retrieval.

## Place in the wider world

These communities belong to Morkahja's wider elemental history. The Live RPG begins earlier, with the peoples living separately and the first playable community centered on the Grimnir.

Elemental bodies, cultural knowledge and shared life connect the peoples to the same world of temporary player inhabitation and persistent consequences.
