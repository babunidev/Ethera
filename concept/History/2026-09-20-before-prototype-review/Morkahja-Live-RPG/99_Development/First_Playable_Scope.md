# First Playable Scope

## Connected interaction prototype

[Mountain Strike](../06_Gameplay/Mountain_Strike.md) now provides the detailed first combat test: eligibility, focused target, Aimed Attacks or random targeting, timing, defensive reaction, binary injury and one resolved event Shard. Echo Attunement exercises shared references and bidirectional selection. Archival stones and Echo Cradles extend this foundation through physical knowledge preservation.

The knowledge model uses one-week ordinary retention, authored Fountain permanence and three-slot archives. A lightweight history holds the latest 100 interactions. These rules guide staged implementation alongside the small playable goals below.

The first playable establishes one Grimnir community: mineral-bodied people becoming individuals through shared work, discovery and care. The atmosphere is quiet, curious and full of the possibility of a civilization beginning.

## Begin with inhabitation

Start with a few simple characters, each with an identity and a small set of Traits and preferences. A player can observe them, inhabit one, move through the settlement, leave them to AI and inhabit another. This establishes the continuing lives at the heart of the game.

Placeholder graphics can be as simple as a moving shape, boxes marked House and lines representing a wall. Space, grass and wind can already suggest the savanna setting. Shaped stone, resonant sound and mineral bodies give later visual work its Grimnir identity.

## Connect the first actions

Build toward a short sequence: move through the village, greet someone, pick up and visibly carry an item, discover a landmark or stone circle, gain a Shard and return its knowledge to the cultural source. One interaction requiring suitable Traits or an item demonstrates how capability, objects and discovery work together.

Characters enter play with ongoing lives and tasks. An adult can continue an available Destiny thread or select another existing thread in the panel. The early experience should make that continuity understandable.

## Grow the community

The working settlement target is 30 Grimnir. Resources, tasks and roles guide refinement of that population. Develop autonomous daily life, meaningful social exchange and cultural memory around the same character framework.

The [first twelve interactions](../06_Gameplay/First_Twelve_Grimnir_Interactions.md) give the initial authored content its four branches. Body-part combat, Shard-backed task guidance and personal development build on the first connected actions. Birth, youth and coming of age extend the community across generations.
