# Settlement Simulation and Tasks

The first settlement is a community of Grimnir with homes, work, knowledge, relationships and developing lives. Its people continue ordinary activity under AI while players inhabit individuals and pursue shared and personal undertakings.

## A secure foundation

The village provides basic nourishment, care and belonging. Food, water, shelter, health, safety and social connection form its daily concerns. Player contributions create surplus, comfort, celebration, growth and opportunities for new people.

People belong even when they cannot work. Care, stories, knowledge, creativity, exploration and companionship all contribute to communal life.

## Work from community needs

The cultural knowledge source offers work suited to a person's current capabilities and the settlement's circumstances. Available Traits, items and knowledge determine the fit. The person's role describes their main qualities, while tasks can draw on the whole range of their abilities.

When immediate provision is secure, useful work can prepare for future growth, improve surroundings or support a ritual. Ordinary participation is broadly accessible; relevant specialists can contribute more effectively. A skilled hunter might return a larger animal or greater yield, while another participant brings a smaller contribution.

## Accepting and following tasks

The character approaches the source and performs the task-acceptance interaction. A cultural animation accompanies acceptance, and the task becomes available for priority selection and guidance.

Existing personal and community tasks can be selected from the common task area. Injury, repair and other new Needs join that list. Important entries draw attention, and the player decides whether to change the current priority.

When the player exchanges knowledge at the fountain, its solution check connects their Needs to available supplies, people, places or newly created content. The resulting Shards help the character pursue the next steps.

## AI participation

Uninhabited people gather, carry, rest, help and take part in society. They can explore within their endurance and return with real objects or Shards that become meaningful to other people's tasks.

Capable AI residents pause routine work to help when approached. Knowledge exchange is a performed cultural interaction. Player-controlled participants choose whether to join.

AI maintains ordinary life; players advance Destiny and initiate reproduction. Community growth follows the people and support created through those undertakings.

## Building and expansion

The settlement expands across surrounding hex terrain as support is needed. Homes can include craft workspaces. The simulation determines needed structures and their locations; people contribute by making, carrying, placing or shaping components.

Construction advances through visible stages. A stone-shaper can prepare a component while others supply material or install it. When the work is complete, the site becomes usable. Existing suitable structures and surplus count toward supporting new lives.

## Grimnir expression

Shaped stone shelters, resonance baths, resting stones, mineral food preparation and a memory-stone knowledge place give the settlement its character. Completed works and new lives can prompt celebration. Touch, rhythm and resonance connect care, work and cultural exchange.

The intended starting society is around thirty Grimnir. Earlier prototype steps can use a few inhabitants to establish the same relationships between life, knowledge, items and work.

## A cooperative day

A resident returns with firewood while another contributes a shaped stone to a building. A traveler pauses to exchange news; a capable worker leaves a routine task long enough to assist with a repair. Their contributions improve conditions for the whole settlement and leave useful knowledge for future inhabitants.
