# Ancient World and Magic

**Current concept through answer 251 · updated 2026-09-13.**

Consolidated concept material, with current rules and unresolved details distinguished.

## Ancient Morkahja

### Era

The game inhabits a very early age of the existing Morkahja world: primal societies, possibly before agriculture, but already living in settlements. The four elemental peoples have not yet encountered one another. The broader concept includes each people, but the first playable version focuses on one Grimnir settlement before expanding.

Small houses, wooden fences or palisades, natural shrines, altars, and stone circles occupy a landscape dominated by wilderness. Prairie and savanna are initial visual impressions, not a restriction to one biome. Rituals may involve animals or other sacred gifts.

### Broader world reference

The supplied world introduction describes a primal world shaped by elemental gods, with living magic, frozen mountain ranges, desert sanctuaries, drifting cities, extraordinary beauty, and the danger of corrupted magic. The four peoples' elemental natures shape their homelands and everyday life.

The source's developed cities and cultural institutions are broader lore references. Their exact early forms in this game's ancient era have not been fixed. Do not automatically populate the starting villages with every later city's institution or technology.

### Still open

The chronology of agriculture, elemental creation, and the arrival or awakening of magic needs reconciliation with the larger world lore. No new historical timeline is invented here.

## Magic

### Character and atmosphere

Magic is powerful, rare, primal, and mysterious. It is not a routine display of conventional spellcasting mages. Shamans, druids, ritualists, stone circles, sacred places, visions, and natural altars fit the intended period.

The game occurs before, or at the beginning of, magic arriving or awakening in the world. Visible energy can pour through terrain and living things, soaking into and flowing out of matter. Subtle distortion and motion make reality feel charged and strange.

### Purpose and integrity

Following destiny reinforces individuality and can sustain or replenish energy. Purposeful participation in community also keeps a being healthy. Aimless wandering eventually brings fatigue and a need to return home.

Weakening integrity can lead to losing the boundary between an individual and their element. Excessive connection to a particular corrupt magic can instead transform a being into something sickening and hostile to the world.

These conditions appear through the body and environment rather than a morality meter or explicit warning popup. See [Diegetic Interface](../08_Presentation/Interface_and_Divine_Orientation.md) and [Death and Dissolution](Atranor_Death_and_Dissolution.md).

### Unresolved rules

Spell lists, costs, corruption thresholds, and the relationship between elemental bodies and magic's historical arrival are not specified.

### Divine Orientation

The gods' view of the world supports current directional guidance separately from mortal knowledge. Signs are subtle and intermittent; they guide the next step without deciding success. One unified system comes first; god-specific styles are deferred. Atranor remains the established name despite later speech-transcription variants. See [Environmental Guidance](../08_Presentation/Interface_and_Divine_Orientation.md).

## Example E04: Primitive places and emerging magic

*Illustrative example from the discussion.*

Small buildings and wooden palisades occupy prairie or savanna-like terrain, without restricting the world to one biome. Natural altars, stone circles, ritual gifts, and powerful rare magic suggest the early age. Energy flowing into and out of terrain gives it a subtly dreamlike instability.

## Earlier world cycles

Rare artifacts in caves can be remnants of a civilization from before the current cycle. This is not Morkahja's first round of existence. The current peoples' young culture therefore coexists with much older remains. Exact previous-cycle history is deferred.
