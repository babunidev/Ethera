# Grimnir

The initial authored action set now has [twelve Grimnir foundations](../06_Gameplay/First_Twelve_Grimnir_Interactions.md) derived from nine named innate capacities. Earlier seven-capacity descriptions below describe physiology rather than a competing final interaction list.

The latest Grimnir social model has permanent Recognition/Sympathy/Trust, no exclusive-family ladder, communal final shaping and child-rearing, and guaranteed basic survival. See [Relationships](../04_Community/Relationships_and_Social_Life.md) and [Birth](../03_Characters/Birth_Youth_and_Adulthood.md).

## The Stonewrought
Formed from stone, metal, and crystal, the Grimnir inhabit the Skylands: vast floating islands shaped into cities that drift above the world.

## Homeland and settlements
Airborne cities are sustained by their communities' work. When Skylands gather, bridges connect them for trade, reunions, and great communal celebrations.

## Community and everyday life
Communities are organized around crafts. Forgers, architects, navigators, and memorykeepers each contribute. Creation is central to life, and an artisan's work can shape the character of an entire city.

## Knowledge traditions from the RPG conversation

Memory stones, architectural records, Memory-Keepers, and traveling Skykeepers preserve and carry knowledge through craft and living memory.

## Place in this game

The ancient setting keeps the peoples separate before they have met. The first playable version begins with one Grimnir community; the others are later expansion scope. The developed settlements described above come from the broader world introduction; their precise early forms remain open. Roles are culture-specific callings assigned at adulthood from traits. Their exact roster has not been chosen.

All peoples participate in the shared character system: 27 latent birth traits, playable youth, adult roles and destinies, temporary player inhabitation, and continuing AI life. Grimnir reproduction is now specified in the character chapters; the exact dissolution rules still need adaptation.

See [Cultural Knowledge Systems](../05_Knowledge/Shards_Memory_and_Cultural_Hubs.md), [Birth and Inheritance](../03_Characters/Birth_Youth_and_Adulthood.md), and [Ancient Morkahja](../01_World/Ancient_World_and_Magic.md).

All four peoples share a broadly humanoid baseline of ordinary actions, with different elemental abilities layered on top. Baseline embodiment may be represented as underlying traits. All elemental baseline abilities are present by playable youth; seven broad Grimnir capacities are now described in the baseline chapter, while specialized action content remains to be defined. Knowledge exchanges and social bonding take place through nearby, culturally appropriate interactions. See [Baseline Actions](../03_Characters/Roles_and_Baseline_Abilities.md).

## First playable culture

The initial playable focus is one Grimnir settlement. The desired feeling is individuality and community beginning to emerge from mindless stone, with mystery, wonder, and a quiet dreamlike tone. Early architecture and role rosters remain open; diet, rest, reproduction, and elemental baseline are now specified in the linked chapters. Later Skyland lore is not the first-settlement design. See [First Playable Scope](../99_Development/First_Playable_Scope.md).




## Early civilization and newly specified life

This game's Grimnir have only recently awakened into individual humanoid consciousness from primal stone forms. There are no Skylands yet. Later floating cities, institutions, architecture, inter-cultural diplomacy, and ancestral traditions remain broader lore, not automatic first-settlement content.

Their food/mineral/water/geomancy physiology, sleep, safe surface rest, stonebound background lore, and care are detailed in [Grimnir Physiology and Care](../03_Characters/Grimnir_Physiology_and_Care.md). Progress-driven reproduction and support-gated youth are in [Birth, Youth and Adulthood](../03_Characters/Birth_Youth_and_Adulthood.md). Seven shared elemental capacities and participation beyond job labels are in [Roles and Baseline Abilities](../03_Characters/Roles_and_Baseline_Abilities.md).

The discussion supplied [extended Grimnir lore](../../Reference_Material/Grimnir_Attached_Lore.md) and an [ability reference with twelve named skills and seven proposed baseline capacities](../../Reference_Material/Grimnir_Ability_Reference.txt). These are preserved in full. The ability reference reports a database audit; this archive does not independently certify that audit. Its recommendations remain proposals where not established in the conversation. In particular, later Skyland imagery and mastered abilities do not become universal ancient-era capabilities.

The extended lore includes the Terran Assembly (Stonara, Ferrumis, Umbrael), the Spiral Forge-Ring, resonance-based geomancy, craft lineages, memory stones and Skykeepers, and later Skyland cultural diversity. These details remain available as reference without requiring their mature forms in the playable beginning.
