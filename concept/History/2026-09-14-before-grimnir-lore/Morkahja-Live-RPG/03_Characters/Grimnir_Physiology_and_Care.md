# Food, minerals, water, and geomancy



## Food, minerals, water, and geomancy

Grimnir bodies are living stone, metal, and crystal. Four processes complement one another:

- Food fuels movement, thought, and internal elemental activity. Roots, grains, mushrooms, fruits, and other foods have flavors and supply energy.
- Minerals replenish bodily substance. Everyday meals support maintenance; growing children and injured adults need more. Making minerals nourishing and delicious is a culinary art.
- Water enables digestion and carries dissolved nutrients and mineral particles through fine internal channels in place of a bloodstream. Stone-bodied Grimnir can become thirsty.
- Geomancy holds their living structure together. Skyland cores belong to later civilization. Earlier starvation examples are superseded by the guaranteed safe gameplay baseline described below.

Their digestive chamber uses grinding, warmth, water, and geomantic activity to break down food. Environmental mineral absorption supports bodily maintenance and repair. Food remains nourishing and culturally meaningful, but the latest baseline permits survival through ordinary environmental and communal provision without a starvation loop.

Hunger and thirst are slow background simulation concerns, not repetitive single-session survival meters or eating after every fight to restore health. Village routines provide nourishment. Hunger may become a situational Need and unlock participation in a prepared communal meal. Such participation can satisfy hunger and provide energy, happiness, recovery, or a temporary Well Fed state. Numerical bonuses and the precise representation of temporary states remain open; these do not silently replace the permanent-trait rule. Providing opportunities for cultural nourishment matters more than manually performing every bite.

## Rest, exhaustion, and affectionate care

Exhaustion works like injury: it can make particular interactions unavailable. A tired Grimnir may lack the focus to understand or take in a new Shard, just as a leg injury may prevent running or climbing. This is a state-dependent interaction restriction, not an assumed universal percentage penalty. Exact blocked-action lists remain content design.

Food restores reserves; rest repairs and realigns internal structures. During sleep, awareness quiets while gentle vibrations redistribute minerals, mend tiny fractures, and restore coordination for movement and geomancy. A sleeping Grimnir may emit a low hum. Dangerous stillness means cessation of internal elemental motion, not lying still or sleeping.

Shaped stone beds with mineral layers carry soothing vibrations. A meal and ordinary sleep support normal recovery; severe exhaustion or injury may need longer care in a mineral-rich resonance bath, an adult counterpart to nursery baths. A companion can rest a hand against someone and match their quiet rhythm to help them settle into sleep. These are cultural care examples, not finalized recovery durations.

## Surface rest and stonebound lore

Wild surface stone is restorative, correcting the earlier suggestion that resting against any wild rock is dangerous. A sun-warmed boulder offers comfort, vibrations, and recovery; prepared village structures are more effective. Sun, wind, rain, growing life, and earth together sustain individuality.

Prolonged life deep underground allows earth to dominate. This is not caused by entering a cave or spending a night underground. Over long residence, movement slows, bodies become heavier and bulkier, awareness turns inward, and individuality can fade toward primal rock-elemental form and apparently motionless stone.

Stonebound transformation is background lore, not a routine player-pressure meter. If a trace of individuality remains and the Grimnir preferences to return, patient treatment and renewed elemental balance can restore awareness and mobility. Longer stonebound residence makes recovery longer. A silent stone may still hold a person; care must respect their remaining will. Its exact relationship to Atranor and final dissolution is not specified.

This explains later Skyland life without placing Skylands in the RPG's beginning. The first settlement belongs to people only recently awakened from boulders and primal earth elementals into self-aware humanoid individuals. They have natural talent for stonework, but are only beginning shared culture and architecture.

## Safe sustenance supersedes starvation pressure

The latest gameplay rule guarantees basic nourishment and bodily maintenance. Minerals from the surroundings, rainwater, and ordinary communal provision form the safe baseline; meals improve well-being and support culture rather than prevent routine starvation. This revises the earlier claim that food deprivation must lead to exhaustion or that mineral absorption can never sustain baseline life. Food, water, minerals, and geomancy remain useful physiological descriptions, but do not create a hunger-driven death loop. Detailed reconciliation of metabolism with the effortless baseline is not a survival mechanic to invent. Tiredness and injury still affect actions; temporary positive states use the rules in [States and buffs](States_Buffs_and_Visible_Condition.md).

## Essence care and lasting body shaping

The first Aid set includes Replenish Essence, expressed through the working Inner Harmony interaction: prayer-like help from companions or the village maintains mind, motivation and individuality. Aimless wandering can deplete Essence; exact rates and relation to fatigue remain unchosen. The Social body-shaping interaction provides a permanent bodily enhancement, analogous to enchantment/gem socketing on the body, rather than a temporary social-event buff. Nourishment and safe communal survival remain distinct from these capabilities. See [first twelve](../06_Gameplay/First_Twelve_Grimnir_Interactions.md).
