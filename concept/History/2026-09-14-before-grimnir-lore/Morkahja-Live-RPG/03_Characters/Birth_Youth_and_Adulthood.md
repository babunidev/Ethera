# Birth Youth and Adulthood

**Current concept through answer 251 · updated 2026-09-13.**

From inherited foundations to simulated early childhood, playable youth, and the adulthood ritual. The current progression rule is participation, not passive elapsed time.

## Birth and Inheritance

Reproduction creates persistent people. A growing community can eventually support more playable lives, but early children are not immediately available for player control. Playability begins in youth, once meaningful participation is possible.

### Trait origin

Draw 9 traits from each parent and 9 from the full trait pool, for 27 latent birth traits. There is no category balancing. The result informs identity and later role rather than serving a preselected build.

Unmet local preference Traits can give counterpart traits priority in future newborn generation. This refines the earlier fully unweighted-random idea while retaining the origin counts. Exact weighting and which draws it affects remain open.

Crafting traits are flat independent qualities that can be inherited, randomly present, or earned through questing. They are not a profession ladder.

### preference Trait origin and formation

The separate 27-Desire target and its 9+9+9 formation schedule are superseded. Persistent preferences are now Traits. How their inheritance and youth expression fit within the retained personal Trait model is open; do not automatically add another 27 Traits. Hidden youth qualities emerge through participation rather than elapsed time.

After development is complete, a final coming-of-age quest and ritual reveal the role and begin adulthood and destiny. Elemental baseline is already available at playable youth.

Grimnir childhood belongs to communal care; creator connections remain origin history rather than an exclusive family network. Grimnir reproduction is defined below. Duplicate traits do not stack; specific inheritance eligibility and whether birth draws must contain distinct traits remain open.

See [Trait Generation](Identity_Traits_Desires_and_Needs.md) and [Adulthood](#adulthood).

## Childhood

### Early childhood: simulated, not playable

Early children exist in the settlement and develop in the background. Direct player control begins in youth, when the individual can meaningfully participate in society. This replaces the earlier playable-childhood design.

### Playable youth

A youth has the full elemental baseline abilities, some expressed personal Traits (including preferences), and no adult social role or ordinary Destiny Crest. They can help, explore, learn, and build relationships. Latent Traits (including preferences) remain completely hidden until they emerge; the panel does not preview the finished adult.

Ordinary village activities, tasks, quests, and social participation develop the youth and reveal remaining Traits (including preferences). Adulthood is progress-based, not a timer or passive aging unlock. All activities can contribute; the entire formation phase is not a separate scripted initiation questline.

The 27 birth-trait pool remains; the separate Desire target is removed. The earlier suggested nine initially active traits is not a finalized youth starting count. Physical presentation can still express development, but elapsed time alone does not grant adulthood.

### Final threshold

When youth development is complete, a final coming-of-age quest leads to a cultural ritual, potentially involving materials or a role-related act. The role is revealed during the ritual. Adulthood and the beginning of destiny occur there together; the final destiny remains concealed until later progress.

Exact rewards, initial youth expression, support thresholds, and whether uninhabited AI can complete the final rite remain open. Early childhood has no fixed duration: settlement support gates playability.

See [Adulthood](#adulthood) and [Baseline Actions](Roles_and_Baseline_Abilities.md).

## Adulthood

### Participation and ritual

Playable youth develops through ordinary community participation, tasks, and questing. After the remaining youth Traits (including preferences) have emerged, a final coming-of-age quest prepares a culturally specific ritual. It may also be related to the future role.

The role is not announced beforehand: it is revealed during the rite. The ritual establishes adulthood, the social role, and the beginning of destiny together.

### A life opening outward

All birth Traits are expressed by adulthood. They include identity and preferences as applicable and inform the character’s calling and possibilities. The role title reflects dominant Traits; it grants no independent abilities or interactions.

Destiny begins through available quests and branches, not an immediate explanation of the final purpose. Later progress gradually reveals the current Crest’s culminating purpose. Routes and final circumstances can differ while that purpose remains stable.

### Continuing growth

Quests add permanent qualities and new possibilities. Crafting specializations are independent traits rather than ranks. Broader capabilities permit more complex subsequent quests without player-facing tiers.

AI maintains communal life but does not advance personal destiny. Adult aging and exact ritual implementation remain open. Role titles update from changed dominant Traits when visiting the fountain.

See [Destiny](../06_Gameplay/Destiny_Quests_and_Progression.md), [Childhood](#childhood), and [Social Roles](Roles_and_Baseline_Abilities.md).

## Grimnir reproduction: two identities and a shared undertaking

Two willing Grimnir contribute fragments of their elemental identities within carefully chosen stone. A third presence develops influences from both creators and its own will. Stone supplies substance, water carries minerals, warmth supports transformation, and air/surface exposure keeps the elemental balance. A sheltered nesting place lets those influences reach the child. Early communication can be faint vibrations responding to familiar touch or a returned rhythm. Birth is recognized when the child sustains its own form and expresses independent will.

Initiating the joining interaction temporarily displaces each parent's ordinary destiny with a **Reproduction Destiny**. The developing identity may take a crystal-like form; that material fragment is not automatically a Knowledge Shard. Development is measured by tending, completed tasks, and experience, not a pregnancy timer.

Both parents receive quest trees that may overlap without being identical. Examples include gathering food/minerals, retrieving knowledge or an essence from a sacred stone circle or temple, preparing the nest, processing supplies, and performing paired rituals or loving resonance that nourishes the new being. Some steps require cooperation; others are individual journeys. Community help can make progress faster.

A stable nesting place with prepared stone and suitable vibrating crystals can sustain the child while both parents travel. Constant bedside supervision is not required. They return to nurture the child and complete the needed interactions. When supplies already exist, creation can progress quickly. When resources are occupied or many families are creating children, longer gathering adventures may be needed; each generated Need must nevertheless have an attainable fulfillment.

At birth the Reproduction Destiny ends and an autonomous, unplayable child joins village life. Players initiate reproduction and drive its progression; capable AI can support ordinary or cooperative tasks. Exact suspended-Crest resumption remains open.

## Settlement support gates playable youth

The young child moves around and participates in village life but becomes playable only when the settlement can support them. Housing and food are explicit examples; other required support is to be specified. There is no fixed childhood duration.

Existing suitable homes and surplus provisions count immediately. Do not demand a newly built house merely because the child is new. If support is already available, the transition to playable youth can be immediate or very rapid. If not, the child remains unplayable until conditions are met. This is a deliberate gameplay rule rather than a literal biological age model.

After reaching playable youth, the existing participation-driven discovery of Traits including preferences and final adulthood rite still apply. Community support for youth and participation for adulthood are separate thresholds.

## Creation becomes community; inheritance stays simple

Only players initiate reproduction; the two initiators require compatible Traits/preference Traits and Trust. Preparatory Needs can gather ingredients, materials, knowledge, and ritual conditions before placing the developing being in a nesting stone basin, followed by further tending.

The final shaping requires several community members. The sequence is two initiators → shared final shaping → child of the community. After the child joins the settlement, no permanent mother/father/co-parent gameplay role remains attached to the two creators. Their contribution persists as origin/history, while communal care replaces exclusive family responsibility. This supersedes the interim suggestion of a permanent co-parent status.

Starting traits remain 9 from each creator plus 9 from the random pool. Community caregiving does not rewrite that foundation; ancestry computation stays unobtrusive rather than explaining every quality to the player. The older nine youth-emergent Desires rule is superseded by the unified Trait model. Participation still develops youth without requiring a causal personality model for every childhood interaction. Preference-Trait generation and integration of earlier local unmet-preference priority remain open.
