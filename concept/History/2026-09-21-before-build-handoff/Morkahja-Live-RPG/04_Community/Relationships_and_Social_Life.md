# Relationships and Social Life

Relationships belong to particular people and grow through performed interactions. Compatible Traits and current Needs create opportunities to greet, exchange knowledge, help, share affection and deepen a bond.

## Recognition, Sympathy and Trust

| Relationship step | Required compatibility |
| --- | --- |
| Recognition | One matching preference-Trait pair |
| Sympathy | Two matching pairs |
| Trust | Three matching pairs |

Matches can run in either direction between the two people. Likes Strong matches Strong; Likes Kind matches Kind. The matching qualities enable the relevant interaction, and performing it advances the relationship in sequence.

Established Recognition, Sympathy and Trust remain. Unmatched people are neutral. A greeting or bow can express Recognition; warmer shared movement or affection can express Sympathy; Trust supports deeper cooperation and appropriate intimate or reproductive interactions.

## Interaction and cooperation

An exchange can involve knowledge, an item, help with work, reassurance or a suitable social action. The people's Traits, Needs, state, relationship and surrounding circumstances determine what is possible.

Capable AI pauses ordinary work to cooperate. A player-controlled partner retains the choice to participate. Cultural interactions carry communication through the world.

## Learning who someone is

Names and personal knowledge are learned through exchange. Greater familiarity gives the character more to recall. In the world, clothes, possessions, posture, gait and behavior communicate visible qualities and condition.

The inhabited player sees their own expressed character information. Knowledge of other people comes through their encounters and shared history.

## Recall Knowledge

Selecting Recall Knowledge on a person opens a small stone disk. It presents the relationship, known facts about that person, information learned from them and knowledge taught to them.

The cursor can subtly reflect Recognition, Sympathy or Trust when focusing on a familiar person. The fuller record remains in the contextual disk.

The character’s separate recent history retains the latest 100 interactions. Shards provide usable knowledge under their holder’s retention rules; authored permanent Fountain records and archival stones preserve selected events over longer periods. Recognition, Sympathy and Trust remain established relationship states. A child’s creation, a rare craft or a shared ritual can be a meaningful event to preserve.

## Shared facts open possibilities

An authored interaction can require a particular shared event alongside Traits, Needs, items, Shards and current state. What two people have done together can therefore matter to what they can do next.

Grimnir upbringing is communal. The two creators remain part of a child's origin history, and the wider community shares care and final shaping.

## A useful meeting

One traveler needs a bear's current location, and another carries a recent sighting. Their encounter enables knowledge exchange. Later, Recall Knowledge can show who supplied that information and what they received in return. A separate compatible social interaction can develop the bond between them.
