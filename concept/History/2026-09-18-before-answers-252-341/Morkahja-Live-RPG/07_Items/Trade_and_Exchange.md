# Trade and Exchange

An exchange connects what one person needs with what another can provide. Items, knowledge, assistance and suitable social actions can all take part.

## A matching opportunity

The actor's and target's Traits, Needs, Shards, items and relationship determine whether an interaction is available. Trading for an object can require fulfilling something the holder needs or values in return.

Knowledge exchange can be useful in either direction. One participant may have a report matching the other's Need. Performing the cultural interaction copies that knowledge to the recipient.

## Linked requirements

The holder's requirement can create another step. A needs the claw carried by B; B needs something A lacks; finding that item leads to C and another exchange. When A can supply a matching requirement, the chain can resolve back toward the claw.

Chains can grow as circumstances require. Each Need completes separately, consumptive steps have sufficient supply, and dependencies remain acyclic.

## Following an object

A report can identify an object's maker, past holder or current possession. At the cultural source, an old lead can become a newer one. The player follows knowledge toward the person who can now provide the object.

Taking an item leaves consequences in the shared world. A previous holder may gain a recovery Need, while someone who witnessed the transfer can carry useful knowledge.

## Cooperation and choice

AI residents help when capable and can participate in matching exchanges. A player-controlled partner decides whether to join. Appropriate reassurance, knowledge, shared work or affection can be meaningful contributions alongside material goods.

The community also provides care as part of belonging. Its safe daily foundation supports the people whose work and journeys make exchanges possible.
