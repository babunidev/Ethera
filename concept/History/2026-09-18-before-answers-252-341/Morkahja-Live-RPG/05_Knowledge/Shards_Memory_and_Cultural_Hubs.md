# Shards, Memory and Cultural Hubs

Knowledge connects people to the changing world. A Shard carries information that can matter to an action: a sighting, a location, an object's maker or holder, a relationship fact or a useful report.

## Observation and exchange

An observed Shard accurately records what was witnessed. Its information can become outdated as the world changes. A character retains that report until they observe or receive a relevant update.

Knowledge is shared through a nearby performed interaction. The source retains their knowledge while the recipient receives a copy. Exchange can be useful in either direction: one person may carry the information the other currently needs.

Characters collect knowledge as part of ordinary life. There is no Shard capacity or burden to manage.

## Updating personal knowledge

A newer relevant report replaces the obsolete personal report after exchange. Mere proximity or an unobserved world event does not perform that update.

For example, one character knows that a bear was by the river. Another has seen it move into a cave. Their exchange supplies the newer information, and the first character's practical knowledge changes accordingly.

## Collective memory

The cultural archive holds contributed knowledge, including historical layers. It can remember a bear sighting, its later death, a vacant cave and a new occupant. For practical guidance, it supplies the newest relevant information it holds.

A world node maintains current contents. Historical copies remain with people and communal memory as that node changes. Sharing at the hub contributes to the community's archive; each other person learns through their own encounter with the knowledge.

The Grimnir consultation source joins local information, community tasks and cultural exchange. Its Memory Fountain name describes that shared function.

## Grimnir attunement

Carved resonance stones carry stories that can be heard through wind, voice and attunement. Memory stones can also preserve ancestral impressions of experience, emotion and craftsmanship. A keeper helps the visitor attend to their patterns and interpret what they encounter.

An ancestral impression carries an individual's perspective. Understanding grows through encounter and reflection. Early communities can preserve recent experiences, while later archives gather the work and memories of generations.

## Tasks and solution Shards

When the character exchanges knowledge at the fountain, the system examines their Needs. It reuses a free valid solution or creates an appropriate one in the world, then supplies the relevant information through a solution Shard.

The chain is **Need → information Shard → solution → interaction**. A healing task might require knowledge of a healing staff, the staff itself, a capable healer and the treatment interaction.

The task's small checklist shows “Acquire information at the Memory Fountain” while the necessary information is unknown. After exchange, it displays the learned requirements or destination.

## Knowledge from making and meeting

Making a spear creates a Shard connecting the maker to the object. That provenance can pass to an informed person or the communal source. Seeing the spear alone reveals its physical presence; learning its maker's identity follows the knowledge chain.

Encounters can produce questions such as “Who made this spear?” or “Who holds it now?” A known older owner can lead to a new report, then to the current holder and an exchange.

Recall Knowledge presents what the inhabited person knows about another individual, including knowledge taught and received. Important shared events remain part of that relationship's history.

## Following a changing lead

A traveler sets out for a bear claw using an old sighting. At the source they learn that someone killed the bear and carried the claw home. The next step becomes meeting that holder. Other useful leads can point to communal stores, a potter, a landmark beyond a split stone or a recently returned scout.

Divine Orientation supports the current useful route through subtle signs while the character's factual knowledge continues to develop through observation and exchange.
