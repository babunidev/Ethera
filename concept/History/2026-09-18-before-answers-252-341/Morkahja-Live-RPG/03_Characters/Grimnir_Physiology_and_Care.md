# Grimnir Physiology and Care

Grimnir bodies are living arrangements of stone, metal and crystal. Nourishment, material renewal, water and geomantic coherence support their movement, thought and growth.

## Nourishment

| Process | Function |
| --- | --- |
| Food | Supports energy, flavor, satisfaction and internal elemental activity. |
| Minerals | Replenish bodily substance and support growth and repair. |
| Water | Carries dissolved nutrients and minerals through fine internal channels. |
| Geomancy | Maintains the coherence of the living structure. |

Meals can combine roots, grain, mushrooms, fruit and other produce with carefully prepared mineral ingredients. Growing children and injured adults benefit from material suited to their condition. Making minerals nourishing and delicious belongs to Grimnir culinary craft.

The digestive chamber works through grinding, warmth, water and geomantic activity. Environmental mineral absorption also supports bodily maintenance and repair.

## A secure daily foundation

The settlement guarantees basic nourishment and bodily maintenance through ordinary communal and environmental provision. Meals enrich well-being and social life. Gathering, cooking and sharing create opportunities for care, enjoyment and helpful temporary states.

## Rest and recovery

During sleep, awareness quiets while gentle vibrations redistribute minerals, mend small fractures and restore coordination. A low hum can express those internal rhythms.

Shaped resting stones carry soothing vibrations through mineral layers fitted to the body. A meal and sleep support ordinary recovery. A mineral-rich resonance bath can provide deeper care after severe exhaustion or injury. A companion can place a hand against someone and match their quiet rhythm as they settle.

Tiredness changes action availability. A person may lack the concentration for a knowledge exchange or the energy for demanding movement. The character panel lists the active condition and its effects.

## Surface stone and elemental balance

A sun-warmed boulder offers a traveler comfort, useful minerals and contact with surrounding life. Earth, wind, rain, warmth and growing things meet at the surface. Prepared settlement structures provide especially comfortable and efficient recovery.

Prolonged residence deep underground allows earth to dominate. Movement can slow, the body become bulkier and heavier, and awareness turn inward toward primal stone. The transformation develops through sustained elemental isolation; everyday cave exploration and overnight shelter remain ordinary activities.

If any individuality remains and the person wishes to return, patient treatment and renewed balance can restore awareness and movement. The longer the stonebound state has lasted, the longer recovery takes.

## Essence and bodily enhancement

Prayer-like replenishment from companions or the village supports Essence: the mind, motivation and coherence of individuality. Aimless wandering can drain it, linking journeys to purpose and communal return.

Body shaping creates lasting enhancements through carefully working the living mineral body. Resonance, touch and craft connect this care to the wider Grimnir material culture.
