### Assistant

**Ward** has a few meanings, which is why it can feel slippery.

In **Everward**, it is not really the noun *ward* as in “a hospital ward” or “someone under guardianship.”

It is the old directional suffix **-ward**, meaning:

**“in the direction of”**  
or  
**“toward.”**

Examples:

- **forward** = toward the front
- **homeward** = toward home
- **inward** = toward the inside
- **outward** = toward the outside
- **skyward** = toward the sky

So **Everward** is best understood as:

**ever + ward = always onward / forever toward**

It is closer to **onward** than to **warden**.

There is also a separate noun **ward** meaning someone or something under protection, or a division/area, and **warden** comes from that protective/guarding family of words. But that is not the meaning active in **Everward**.

## Ethera

**Ethera** is the current development name for the game set in the world of **Morkahja**.

The game needed a name that could stand on its own while still feeling deeply rooted in Morkahja.

### Why Ethera

The name emerged naturally from **Ethera’s Vale**, the central region of Morkahja that later becomes associated with Aetherion, the great multicultural city where the different peoples and elemental traditions eventually meet.

This gives the title a sense of depth without forcing its meaning to be explained immediately.

**Ethera** also carries the right sound for the game: soft, ancient, slightly mysterious, and broad enough to hold the entire world experience rather than one specific mechanic or culture.

It avoids making the game sound primarily about dreams, destiny, memory, combat, or any single element. Those things all exist within the game, but none of them alone define it.

### The game structure

The first version of the game focuses entirely on the **Grimnir**.

This is not intended to remain a Grimnir-only game. The long-term structure is based around the four elemental peoples of Morkahja, with each culture eventually receiving its own fully developed playable experience.

**Ethera: Stonewrought**
