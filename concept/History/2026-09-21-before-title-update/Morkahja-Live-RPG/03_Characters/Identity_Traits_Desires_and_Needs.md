# Identity, Traits, Preferences and Needs

Every character belongs to the world. Their body, life stage, knowledge, relationships, possessions and ongoing tasks persist across player and AI control.

## Traits

Traits are discrete qualities describing abilities, appearance, personality and preferences. A character either has a Trait or does not. Permanent personal Traits remain once acquired; temporary condition and equipment determine how they can currently be used.

Strong, Funny, Loved, Fast, Good Climber, Good Speaker, Green Thumb, Knows Herbs and Knows Language illustrate this breadth. A visible quality such as Strong should affect the person's form and behavior. Broad expertise belongs to Traits; knowledge of a particular bear's location belongs to a Shard.

Traits combine horizontally. Crafter, Leatherworker and Cloak Crafter can all contribute to the same craft. Duplicate sources still represent one quality: Kind + Kind = Kind.

## Preferences

Persistent attractions are Traits such as Likes Strong, Likes Kind and Likes Sacred. A person can possess both Strong and Likes Strong. A missing preference is neutral.

Matching is exact. Likes Kind on one person and Kind on another can enable a fitting social interaction. Preferences can concern people, creatures, objects or places. A Soft cloak can matter to someone with the corresponding preference even when its other qualities are irrelevant to that match.

## Birth and development

A new person begins with 27 latent personal Traits: nine drawn from each creator and nine from the allowed personal pool. The distribution can vary freely across Combat, Trickery, Aid and Social.

Playable youth reveals latent qualities through ordinary participation. All birth Traits are expressed by adulthood. The final youth ritual begins Destiny and reveals the person's first social role.

Later, fully completing a Destiny branch grants a random unowned Trait from that branch's pool. This broadens future possibilities. A completed pool instead yields a non-Trait reward from the general reward pool.

## Needs

A Need is a current requirement with an owner and a means of fulfillment. Injury can produce a healing Need; a broken tool can produce a repair Need; a task can require information, materials, a person or a performed interaction.

Each Need has its own completion state. One Need can offer several alternative solutions, while a task can group multiple requirements. Separate consumptive Needs require enough supply for each fulfillment.

Players can use existing knowledge, items, crafting or help. Consulting the fountain searches for a free valid solution and creates one where necessary. A Shard supplies the relevant information for the journey.

## Body, equipment and context

Foundational qualities such as Grimnir, Humanoid and Earth Elemental participate in the same matching language as personal Traits. An object's properties can contribute conditional capabilities while the person carries or equips it. Injury can remove an action that depends on a healthy limb while the underlying expertise remains.

The current action set emerges from the whole situation: actor, target, Traits, Needs, Shards, items, bodily condition, relationships and surroundings.

## A person built from qualities

A Strong, Funny person who knows herbs has physical, social and practical possibilities. Meeting someone who Likes Funny can reveal a social interaction. Finding an injured traveler can make their knowledge useful, while the actual treatment also depends on its tools, materials and other requirements.

## Capability and identity in the first test

Khar-Morr the First Carved is a female Grimnir with Grimnir, Weapon Shaping and Echo Imprint Trait. Her minimal configuration supplies the tested interactions, four healthy body regions and an empty Small Weapon slot. As the test cast grows, earlier configurations remain available for repeatable checks. [Milestone 1](../99_Development/Milestone_1_Khar_Morr.md) defines the starting data.
