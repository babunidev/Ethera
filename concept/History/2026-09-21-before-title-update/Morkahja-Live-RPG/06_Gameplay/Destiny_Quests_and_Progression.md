# Destiny, Quests and Progression

Destiny begins with adulthood and continues through successive **Crests**, each converging on a **Knot**. The Knot brings an undertaking's purpose to culmination and opens the next Crest.

## The Crest

Four approaches begin the structure: **Combat, Trickery, Aid and Social**. Choices expand, then converge:

**4 → 8 → 16 → 8 → 4 → 1 Destiny Knot**

A branch can offer different uses of a person's capabilities. With a small Trait set, two opportunities can vary by creature, place or circumstances. As the person develops, further combinations become possible.

One route is sufficient to reach the Knot. The player can develop other available branches before completing it, and switching approaches retains existing progress. Completing prerequisites opens the next slots on the stone board.

## Purpose and changing preparation

A Knot has a central purpose. Its preparation, materials, rituals and other Needs respond to the person and world. Obtaining a spear can make a spear-based component useful; losing it can change the route.

Current Traits, Needs, possessions and circumstances drive the selection of authored quest pieces. Each step develops from the current situation and preceding outcomes. The immediate opportunities become visible as the character reaches them.

## Branch rewards

Fully completing a branch unlocks one random unowned Trait from its pool. Combat supplies Combat Traits, Trickery supplies Trickery Traits, Aid supplies Aid Traits, and Social supplies Social Traits.

One finished branch earns one Trait; two earn one from each; all four earn four. If the character already possesses the complete pool for a branch, its reward comes from the general non-Trait pool: an item, Shard or other suitable reward.

The fallback is random and can happen to help another person. Rewards preserve existing world objects and their ongoing journeys.

## Community culmination

The Knot brings learning and experience home through a major ritual, preparation, a reveal and communal celebration. The next Crest begins immediately, carrying the life into another undertaking.

Only players advance personal Destiny. Reproduction temporarily gives the two creators Reproduction Destinies whose tasks support a new life.

## Side quests and preparation

An active objective can gain preparatory Needs and detours. If another person holds the required item, meeting them becomes a step. Their requested exchange can lead to another material, person or action.

Existing objects and deeds count when they fit. A claw, pelt or other evidence collected earlier can answer a later requirement. Each separate Need retains its own completion state and sufficient consumable supply.

Failure can lead to treatment, repair, recovery of an object or pursuit of a changed target. Those routes remain attainable through appropriate content and preparation.

## Intersecting journeys

One person needs a bear's claw, another its heart and another food. Their objectives meet at the same existing creature and can bring them together. If the bear has already died, their routes follow its remains or the people carrying the relevant objects.

A person who has explored Social can later pursue Combat or Trickery. The expanded capabilities can enrich subsequent tasks and the expression of the Knot, while their earlier progress remains part of the life.
