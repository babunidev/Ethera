# Multiplayer, Characters and Handoff

Players inhabit available people in one shared world. The world's population and current occupancy determine which lives can be selected.

## Choosing a life

Selection presents the character's known identity, expressed qualities, adult role where applicable, current situation and revealed Destiny direction. Playable youth is the earliest stage available for direct control.

Each person retains their Traits, Needs, Shards, relationships, possessions and unfinished tasks across sessions. Choosing them means continuing their life from those circumstances.

## Queuing and spectating

Players can queue for ordinary availability or also opt into temporary stand-in opportunities. An offer shows the person, their current situation and whether the opportunity is temporary.

Offers go to a small group of the oldest eligible queued players. Recipients can accept or decline during the response window. Once the offer resolves, one eligible accepting player receives exclusive control and the others remain queued.

While waiting, spectators choose an on-screen character to follow and inspect available character information and lore.

## Shared adventures

Groups emerge through overlapping circumstances. One character needs a bear claw, another its heart and another food. Their routes bring them together around the same creature and its continuing consequences.

Knowledge, Traits, Needs and relationships enable local interactions. People exchange reports, assist, trade and cooperate through authored cultural actions. Player text and voice chat are absent.

## Manual departure

Logging out deliberately returns the person to AI control and starts their journey home. Ordinary community participation continues when they arrive.

## Unexpected disconnect

After an unexpected connection loss, the character initially waits. AI considers remaining endurance and the distance home, departing before further waiting would make the return excessively tiring.

An opted-in stand-in can continue the person's shared adventure. With no queued replacement, the character can remain reserved during the waiting period.

## Returning and reclaiming

The disconnected player has approximately ten minutes to reclaim the person. A return within that window notifies the stand-in and transfers control back, with the stand-in returning to the queue.

After the window expires, the stand-in becomes the session's current player and the earlier player returns through ordinary availability.

## A continuing encounter

A player disconnects during a shared journey. A queued stand-in receives the character's recap and continues alongside the others. If the original player returns within the reclaim window, the handoff restores their control without replacing the character or undoing the journey.
