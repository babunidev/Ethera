# Open Questions

**Current concept through answer 104 · reorganized 2026-09-10.**

Consolidated concept material, with current rules and unresolved details distinguished.

## Latest unanswered question

Question 105: does Divine Orientation work only for an active Need/task, or can it also respond to broader destiny without a specific active task? No answer was present at the archived endpoint.

## Character formation

Exact starting youth expression, early-childhood simulation duration, trait/desire reveal rewards, inherited-desire parental split, duplicate trait handling, and final rite requirements remain open. All elemental baseline abilities are confirmed by playable youth; adulthood is participation-based and marked by a ritual. Whether AI can complete that rite remains unspecified.

## Generation and recovery

How local unmet-Desire priority affects the 9+9+9 trait draws is undecided. So are technical solvability checks, cycle handling, vanished exchange partners, destroyed requirements, and final destiny completion after convergence. The policy is established: no permanently closed route from failure, and every Need has a possible solution.

## Craft, equipment, and interactions

Exact trait-preservation counts, crafting input selection, action tests, repairs, carrying attachments, occupied-hand behavior, and equipment capability lifetimes need detail. There is no profession hierarchy, random craft quality roll, or hidden compatibility-success percentage.

Discovery trigger precision, contextual bindings, maximum displayed actions, and accessible execution feedback remain open. Undiscovered interactions are fully hidden; known interactions remain documented with blockers.

## Community and initial scope

Start with one Grimnir settlement. Population size, specific roles, diet, rituals, architecture, and first content set are deferred. Suitable tasks and cooperative adaptation are established; simultaneous task limits and AI task invitations are not.

## Continuing open implementation

Combat timing and multiple opponents, injury/healing details, Atranor transitions, server scale, offline speed, queue timings, spectator information limits, property rules, platform, and age-specific/adult-content boundaries remain open.

## Deferred rather than undecided foundation

Distinct god-specific guidance styles are later ideas. Four-culture simultaneous launch, early-childhood player control, passive timed adulthood, operational task-selection panels, and a visible quest-tier system are not current requirements.
