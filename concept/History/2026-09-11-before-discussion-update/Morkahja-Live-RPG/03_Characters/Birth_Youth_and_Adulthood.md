# Birth Youth and Adulthood

**Current concept through answer 104 · reorganized 2026-09-10.**

From inherited foundations to simulated early childhood, playable youth, and the adulthood ritual. The current progression rule is participation, not passive elapsed time.

## Birth and Inheritance

Reproduction creates persistent people. A growing community can eventually support more playable lives, but early children are not immediately available for player control. Playability begins in youth, once meaningful participation is possible.

### Trait origin

Draw 9 traits from each parent and 9 from the full trait pool, for 27 latent birth traits. There is no category balancing. The result informs identity and later role rather than serving a preselected build.

Unmet local Desires can give counterpart traits priority in future newborn generation. This refines the earlier fully unweighted-random idea while retaining the origin counts. Exact weighting and which draws it affects remain open.

Crafting traits are flat independent qualities that can be inherited, randomly present, or earned through questing. They are not a profession ladder.

### Desire origin and formation

Target 27 Desires: 9 inherited total, 9 generated at birth, 9 experiential. The split between parents remains undecided. Hidden youth traits/desires emerge through community activities and quests rather than elapsed time.

After development is complete, a final coming-of-age quest and ritual reveal the role and begin adulthood and destiny. Elemental baseline is already available at playable youth.

Family and parental contacts may form a starting network. Reproduction details, duplicate traits, and specific inheritance eligibility remain open.

See [Trait Generation](Identity_Traits_Desires_and_Needs.md) and [Adulthood](#adulthood).

## Childhood

### Early childhood: simulated, not playable

Early children exist in the settlement and develop in the background. Direct player control begins in youth, when the individual can meaningfully participate in society. This replaces the earlier playable-childhood design.

### Playable youth

A youth has the full elemental baseline abilities, some expressed personal traits and desires, and no adult social role or destiny. They can help, explore, learn, and build relationships. Latent traits and desires remain completely hidden until they emerge; the panel does not preview the finished adult.

Ordinary village activities, tasks, quests, and social participation develop the youth and reveal remaining traits and desires. Adulthood is progress-based, not a timer or passive aging unlock. All activities can contribute; the entire formation phase is not a separate scripted initiation questline.

The 27 birth-trait pool and 27-desire target remain. The earlier suggested nine initially active traits is not a finalized youth starting count. Physical presentation can still express development, but elapsed time alone does not grant adulthood.

### Final threshold

When youth development is complete, a final coming-of-age quest leads to a cultural ritual, potentially involving materials or a role-related act. The role is revealed during the ritual. Adulthood and the beginning of destiny occur there together; the final destiny remains concealed until later progress.

Exact rewards, initial youth expression, early-childhood duration, and whether uninhabited AI can complete the final rite remain open.

See [Adulthood](#adulthood) and [Baseline Actions](Roles_and_Baseline_Abilities.md).

## Adulthood

### Participation and ritual

Playable youth develops through ordinary community participation, tasks, and questing. After the remaining youth traits and desires have emerged, a final coming-of-age quest prepares a culturally specific ritual. It may also be related to the future role.

The role is not announced beforehand: it is revealed during the rite. The ritual establishes adulthood, the social role, and the beginning of destiny together.

### A life opening outward

All birth traits are expressed by adulthood, alongside the formed desire set and experiential qualities. Traits and desires inform the character's calling and possibilities. Roles give distinctive abilities and interactions without replacing ordinary baseline actions.

Destiny begins through available quests and branches, not an immediate explanation of the final purpose. Later progress gradually reveals the single final purpose. Routes and final circumstances can differ while that purpose remains stable.

### Continuing growth

Quests add permanent qualities and new possibilities. Crafting specializations are independent traits rather than ranks. Broader capabilities permit more complex subsequent quests without player-facing tiers.

AI maintains communal life but does not advance personal destiny. Adult aging, role reassignment, and exact ritual implementation remain open.

See [Destiny](../06_Gameplay/Destiny_Quests_and_Progression.md), [Childhood](#childhood), and [Social Roles](Roles_and_Baseline_Abilities.md).
