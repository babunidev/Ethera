# Roles and Baseline Abilities

**Current concept through answer 104 · reorganized 2026-09-10.**

Consolidated concept material, with current rules and unresolved details distinguished.

## Social Roles

Roles are culture-specific callings assigned at adulthood from a person's traits. The four peoples begin separately and do not use one universal class list. The definitive role list is intentionally deferred.

### Mechanical meaning

A role grants special abilities, acts, knowledge, and unique interactions. It can change what a character perceives. The explicit example is a hunter who can track prey and see tracks in the environment.

Certain roles can unlock interactions with another person, or shared actions for both participants. The comparison to Dota heroes concerns distinct capabilities, not player selection of a fixed hero build.

### Narrative meaning

Role helps shape destiny and side quests. Some rewards should reinforce that calling while still broadening the person. A hunter's story should make sense for their abilities and culture rather than feel randomly unrelated.

### Cultural foundations

The broader lore includes Rokur smithing and communal work; Verdani healing, waterkeeping, and gardening; Aevis learning, architecture, and crystal craft; Grimnir forging, architecture, navigation, and memorykeeping. These provide foundations, not an approved ancient-era playable class roster.

Roles modify an existing humanoid and elemental action baseline; ordinary action is not locked behind a role. Traits, Desires, Needs, Shards, equipment, and injuries together determine meaningful interactions. Role-based activity may also influence emerging Desires, with youth development completed before the adulthood rite. See [Interaction System](../06_Gameplay/Interactions_Discovery_and_Player_Skill.md).

### Revelation and flat craft traits

The adulthood ritual reveals the role; youths do not preview their adult calling. Inhabiting an adult means accepting that role for the session, with world actions rather than activity menus.

Crafter, Leatherworker, and Cloak Crafter are independent traits, not a hierarchical promotion ladder. Relevant overlapping craft qualities contribute together. Examples of hunters, mothers, shamans, and craftspeople illustrate inhabitation; the initial Grimnir role roster is not fixed.

## Baseline Actions

Characters have ordinary embodied actions without requiring a personal trait for every attempt. Broadly humanoid bodies share a common foundation, with different elemental abilities layered on top.

### Availability

All elemental baseline abilities are available when the person becomes playable in youth. Early childhood is simulated rather than player-controlled. This resolves the previous question about waiting for adulthood to unlock the baseline.

Shared humanoid baseline → elemental baseline → personal traits and desires → role → equipment → current state/injuries → usable contextual actions.

Baseline embodiment can be represented as underlying traits, but no rule deducts it from the 27 personal birth traits. Personal qualities expand possibilities; injury can restrict actions without removing permanent traits.

### Equipment and execution

A character can physically use or wear equipment without a matching build. Compatibility unlocks more of its interaction potential. Equipment-granted capabilities are conditional on the equipment and do not automatically become permanent birth/acquired traits.

Character qualities determine available interactions; player skill determines their execution. Tests vary by interaction rather than always requiring timing.

Exact basic action lists, elemental abilities, physical fitting rules, and input bindings remain open.

See [Equipment and Carrying](../07_Items/Items_Equipment_and_Carrying.md) and [Interaction Discovery](../06_Gameplay/Interactions_Discovery_and_Player_Skill.md).

## Example E20: The hunter sees a different forest

*Illustrative example from the discussion.*

Tracking prey reveals signs another person may miss. The role grants actions and perception, compared to the distinct skills of a Dota hero, rather than just a percentage bonus. Role-related side quests can add adjacent knowledge and capabilities.

## Example E21: No walking trait or breathing profession required

*Illustrative example from the discussion.*

Everyone can walk, breathe, and touch through ordinary embodiment. Roles, equipment, and personal traits add possibilities; injuries restrict them. The four peoples share a humanoid foundation but have different elemental actions.
