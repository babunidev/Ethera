# Crafting and Item Traits

**Current concept through answer 104 · reorganized 2026-09-10.**

How material and tool traits become crafted qualities through flat, overlapping expertise, with deterministic results.

## Physical inputs and predictable outputs

Crafting uses the same trait system as the rest of the world. Materials and tools contribute qualities, while the crafter's relevant traits and the performed interaction determine which qualities reach the finished object.

Results are deterministic: the same relevant person, inputs, tools, and action give a reliable result. There is no random quality or critical-craft roll. Knowledge of the process is therefore useful and dependable.

## Flat, cumulative expertise

Crafter, Leatherworker, and Cloak Crafter are independent qualities. There is no prerequisite ladder from general to specific. Each can be inherited, randomly included at birth, or earned through relevant questing.

All applicable traits contribute together. A character with all three contributes all three when making a leather cloak; possessing one gives that one's contribution. This supersedes the intermediate suggestion of hierarchical specialization. The exact number of input traits preserved per contribution is not fixed.

## Quality means compatibility

A richer object carries more relevant input/tool qualities, such as Soft, Warm, Durable, or Fine. This creates more possible Desire or Need matches. A Soft cloak fits someone seeking Soft even if its other qualities are irrelevant to them.

More qualities do not mean a universally better level or larger stat number. The result is useful in more situations and to more people.

## Scarcity and presentation

Clothing requires materials, care, labor, and craft; it is rare and costly in communal effort. It may break and may need repair or replacement. The exact repair rules remain open.

Crafting takes place through world interactions rather than a separate recipe-selection spreadsheet. Discoveries arise through normal play. Detailed recipes, preservation rules, timing, and skill tests remain to be designed.

See [Equipment and Carrying](Items_Equipment_and_Carrying.md) and [Interaction System](../06_Gameplay/Interactions_Discovery_and_Player_Skill.md).

## Example E57: Three relevant qualities contribute

*Illustrative example from the discussion.*

Crafter contributes to crafting generally; Leatherworker contributes when the material is leather; Cloak Crafter contributes when the object is a cloak. A person with all three gets each contribution, without prerequisite ranks or a random quality roll.

## Example E58: A soft cloak is useful to the right person

*Illustrative example from the discussion.*

Inputs and tools can produce Soft, Warm, Durable, and Fine. A person desiring Soft matches because it is soft; other qualities may not matter to them. More qualities create more possible matches. A cloak requested for someone else is a situational task Need, while a personal preference may be a Desire.

## Example E59: Universal usability, richer compatibility

*Illustrative example from the discussion.*

Someone may wear a cloak or use a bow without special matching qualities. A better match opens more potential. The momentary idea of compatibility changing failure chance was resolved toward new actions and player skill instead.
