# Trade and Exchange

**Current concept through answer 202 · updated 2026-09-12.**

How a required object becomes a reciprocal exchange and can create a chain of new Needs.

## Exchanges emerge from actual requirements

A character's Need leads to another person who has the required item or information. Their matching state unlocks the relevant interaction. A trade involves an exchange: the seeker must also satisfy a Need or Desire of the holder with something they can provide.

If the seeker already has a suitable counterpart, the exchange can proceed. Otherwise the situation generates a new objective to obtain what the other person wants.

## Example

Need a bear claw → learn the bear died → find the person with the claw → discover their requirement → obtain what satisfies it → return and exchange → fulfill the original Need.

The new requirement may lead to another person and another exchange. Chains may continue indefinitely; there is no artificial depth cap in the current design. Eventually a character may already possess what is needed and resolve the chain back toward its origin.

## World continuity

The claw remains the existing world object. Routing follows changes in knowledge and possession; it does not respawn a bear or delete the claw merely to complete a quest.

Exchange chains are expressed through current Needs and relevant Shards, not a player-facing full dependency graph. The player may pursue another available thread through in-world actions and return later.

Pricing, currencies, offers between two players, item handover details, cyclic dependencies, and recovery if a link disappears are not yet specified. Ordinary shard sharing should not automatically be converted into paid trade; its copying rule remains separate.

See [Objectives and Side Quests](../06_Gameplay/Destiny_Quests_and_Progression.md) and [Objects and Persistence](Items_Equipment_and_Carrying.md).

## Recoverable branches

Every new exchange Need must have a possible solution. Failed attempts must not permanently close a route. Another branch remains possible, and the panel allows changing the prioritized existing task. Each Crest converges on a Knot before further Crests; exchange chains must remain acyclic with sufficient fulfillment for every separate Need.

## Example E60: An exchange can grow into an adventure

*Illustrative example from the discussion.*

A knows the claw is held by B. B needs something A lacks. Acquiring that thing leads to another person and another requirement. Eventually A may already have what someone needs, allowing the chain to resolve backward. There is no fixed depth limit, but each Need must have a possible solution.

## Social exchanges and recovery

A missing item can generate a recovery chain through the fountain or an informed person, then an interaction with the holder. AI helps if capable and may request a matching Need/Desire fulfillment in return, including knowledge, task help, or appropriate affection rather than only goods. Ordinary tasks are cooperative; player-controlled recipients retain a choice.
