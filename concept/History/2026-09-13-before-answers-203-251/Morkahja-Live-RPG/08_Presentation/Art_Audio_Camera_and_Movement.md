## Visual Direction

### Style

A 3D, dry, restrained, serious fantasy aesthetic. World of Warcraft Classic is a readability reference, but the desired result is less comic and exaggerated. Morrowind is an atmospheric reference for ancient, strange, intense fantasy. The tone leans darker rather than lighthearted, without requiring grimdark.

Stylization should leave room for imagination. Slight distortion, flowing energies, and movement through terrain can suggest magic soaking into and out of the world. Avoid making every mystery visually explicit.

### Setting

Small primitive settlements, wooden boundaries, natural shrines, stone circles, and broad wilderness. Prairie and savanna are starting impressions, with multiple biomes intended. The supplied people's homelands and materials inform cultural identity; their mature city forms are not automatically the ancient settlement designs.

### Embodied condition

Thriving beings may shine, grow more defined, or appear stronger. Weakening beings may shrink, thin, dim, become transparent, or lose definition. Their bodies communicate well-being directly.

Detailed palettes, models, animation language, and production targets remain open.

### Quiet beginning and visible loadout

The first Grimnir community should feel dreamlike, curious, and wondrous: individuality emerging from stone at the birth of a great civilization. Keep stimulation low. An apparently empty landscape reveals small details through attention, rather than filling the view with enemies or spectacle.

Carried equipment, bags, and large objects change the character's silhouette. Clothing expresses real scarcity and craft rather than freely selected cosmetics. See [Equipment and Carrying](../07_Items/Items_Equipment_and_Carrying.md).

## Audio and Voice

### Final communication decision

There is no proximity voice chat and no free player text chat. The earlier voice-chat proposal was explicitly withdrawn because a real player's voice may not fit the different bodies, ages, cultures, and personalities they inhabit.

Players communicate through contextual actions, signals, gestures, movement, role-specific abilities, and cultural knowledge exchange. These interactions depend on actual traits, roles, relationships, and matching information rather than a shallow generic emote list.

### Sound in the world

Sound remains part of immersion. Music, communal gatherings, and cultural activities matter to social gameplay; the source lore also includes Verdani music and festivals and Rokur hearth storytelling.

The absence of player voice chat does not establish silence or prohibit character vocalization. Voice acting, nonverbal vocal sounds, musical direction, subtitles, and the presentation of storytelling have not been decided.

## Camera and Movement

The game is 3D with an elevated perspective currently favored over an over-the-shoulder camera. Dota 2 is a key control and perspective reference; Baldur's Gate 2 and Icewind Dale were also named as perspective references.

Movement should be direct, precise, physical, and responsive. Click-to-move and freely orbiting character-anchored camera control are now chosen below; exact angles, distances, and camera gestures remain to be tuned. The references express a direction rather than a complete control specification.

### Spectator camera

Queued spectators can select visible characters and follow their lives. No free-flying camera is allowed under the latest decision. The earlier free-flight suggestion is superseded.

This restricts observation to existing lives rather than giving a player an independent scouting camera. The exact information available while following, and rules for switching targets, remain open.

Combat refines movement: engaging locks onto the opponent and the game handles natural facing and most positioning. The player chooses timely available responses. Disengaging releases that lock for independent movement or escape. This is a combat-specific refinement of direct control; it does not change the follow-only spectator camera. See [Combat](../06_Gameplay/Combat_and_Injuries.md).

## Example E63: Why player voice was removed

*Illustrative example from the discussion.*

The same player may inhabit different bodies, roles, ages, and cultures. Their real voice may not fit those lives. Proximity voice was therefore withdrawn and free text chat excluded; culturally appropriate actions carry communication. This does not mean environmental audio or character vocalization is forbidden.

## Chosen movement and character-anchored camera

Primary movement is click-to-move, specifically right click under the later interaction binding. Use an elevated angled view, typically closer than Dota so natural details remain visible while the landscape feels large. Dungeon Siege and Sacred are additional references. Constant WASD strafing was rejected as too action-oriented for this pace.

The player freely rotates/orbits around the moving character, including side views or looking back along the route. The chosen angle stays; no forced return to a preferred orientation. Allow a broad zoom range, close for details and far for scale, while remaining aimed at the character. Do not permit panning away to scout independently. Very close first-person-like framing for screenshots is an optional idea, not essential first scope.

The camera gesture must coexist with hold-left-click interaction focus; exact drag bindings, collision, zoom limits, and occlusion handling remain open. Spectating stays character-following rather than free-flying.
