# Game Loop and Sessions

The common task slot defaults to the oldest personal Need, then community needs. Destiny uses the stone board. Village buffs can last for outings and consume a use immediately on return. See [States and buffs](../03_Characters/States_Buffs_and_Visible_Condition.md).

**Current concept through answer 202 · updated 2026-09-12.**

The immediate rhythm of play, a complete session, and the generational cycle in one place.

## Core Game Loop

### A session

Choose an available life and accept its role → orient in the ongoing situation → move and observe → notice signs and details → perform contextual actions → discover, cooperate, craft, or pursue an objective → return with physical goods and knowledge → leave the character to continuing world life.

The moment-to-moment rhythm is **move → observe → notice → interpret → interact → continue**. Quiet discovery matters more than speed or collecting large amounts of loot.

### Purpose and tasks

Accepted tasks and active Needs receive subtle Divine Orientation. The knowledge fountain supplies relevant knowledge and suitable simulation-generated community work through direct cultural interactions. The panel explains the character and discovered actions and lets the player prioritize an existing task or quest thread. New tasks are accepted through world interactions.

Failure creates recoverable detours and new Needs with possible solutions. Players can follow other available destiny approaches through play, without permanently losing branch options.

### The generational cycle

World → community → birth → simulated early childhood → playable youth and participation → coming-of-age ritual → role and destiny → expanding and converging adventures → persistent objects, knowledge, and communal conditions for future people.

Only players advance destiny; AI maintains ordinary life. The first playable focus is one Grimnir settlement. Population contributes playable slots once individuals reach the playable phase.

See [Session Structure](#session-structure) and [First Playable Scope](../99_Development/First_Playable_Scope.md).

## Session Structure

### Inhabit a life

Select an available character and accept their place in the world. The major choice of experience is made here: a role shapes what the player comes to do. Selection presents only the person's currently known situation, not the unrevealed adult role or final destiny.

### Immediate rhythm

Orient → move → observe → notice → investigate → interact → continue.

The world is quiet and apparently sparse, with small signs and discoveries rather than visible mobs everywhere. A sound in a bush, a bird, a glint, a track, or a useful object can matter. Traits change what is noticed and possible.

Meet people, exchange knowledge, greet someone, deepen a bond, continue an objective, or perform suitable community work. Branches can change through play; there is no recurring menu asking which activity genre to do next.

### Purpose and return

Divine signs provide direction without constant stimulation. The journey is meaningful observation rather than empty travel. Carrying is physical, limiting what comes home. Return to share and refresh knowledge, deliver useful goods, and participate in community recovery.

Purpose and community participation sustain a being; aimless travel can tire them. Sessions remain voluntary without a fixed required duration. Long dependency chains need not be finished in one session.

### Departure

Manual logout sends the character home under AI. Unexpected disconnect first waits, with return before excessive fatigue and optional queued stand-ins under existing rules. Account diary remains optional and grants no gameplay power.

See [Exploration](../06_Gameplay/Exploration.md) and [Logout and Handoff](../06_Gameplay/Multiplayer_Characters_and_Handoff.md).

## Example E02: A short session can still contribute

*Illustrative example from the discussion.*

Inhabit a person already seeking a plant in the woods. Read signs, investigate a glint, meet someone with relevant knowledge, and return with a new sighting or useful object. The session has contributed even if the final destiny was not completed.
