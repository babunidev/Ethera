# Atranor Death and Dissolution

**Current concept through answer 154 · updated 2026-09-11.**

Consolidated concept material, with current rules and unresolved details distinguished.

## Atranor

Atranor is the place of divine judgment when a being has exhausted their godly essence or fallen out of divine favor. Death is ultimately decided by the gods.

### Outcomes

- **Continuation:** the gods replenish the individual's essence and permit the same person to continue their journey toward destiny. The user clarified this as continuing life, rather than becoming a newly resurrected identity.
- **Dissolution:** the gods refuse continuation. The individual vanishes and becomes one with everything.

The dissolved being's essence may be repurposed. Their role and destiny return to the pool from which new characters are made. This does not establish a player-owned reincarnation or inherited account advantage.

### Still open

The appearance and geography of Atranor, whether judgment is playable, criteria used by the gods, frequency of second chances, and links between exhaustion, elemental dissolution, and corruption have not been fully defined.

## Death and Dissolution

### Divine judgment

Death is ultimately decided by the gods. When godly essence is exhausted, the being is brought to Atranor. The gods either replenish essence and allow the same person to continue or deny continuation and dissolve the individual.

On dissolution, the being becomes one with everything. Their essence may be repurposed and their role and destiny return to the generative pool for future people. No account-owned inheritance is established.

### Elemental loss of individuality

Insufficient reinforcement of individual identity can dissolve the boundary between person and element. The example given is a Verdani becoming water. The other peoples have their own elemental expressions, but no complete transformation catalogue was supplied.

### Corruption

Excessive connection with corrupt magic can turn a being into something harmful to the world, potentially a hostile creature others must put down. This is distinct from simply returning to a pure element.

### Presentation and unresolved links

Bodies reveal weakening through diminished definition, size, solidity, or brightness. The game does not rely on explicit warning popups. The precise relationship between elemental dissolution, hostile corruption, physical defeat, essence exhaustion, and judgment at Atranor still needs one consistent ruleset.

Combat now uses broad functional injuries. Being too injured to fight changes available actions and can lead to surrender, escape, capture, or help rather than immediate death. AI entities obey the same state logic. The connection between these outcomes and essence exhaustion or divine judgment remains open; no replacement for Atranor's established role is implied. See [Injuries](../06_Gameplay/Combat_and_Injuries.md).

*Illustrative example from the discussion.*
