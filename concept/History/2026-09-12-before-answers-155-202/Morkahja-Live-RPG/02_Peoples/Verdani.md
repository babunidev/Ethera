# Verdani

**Current concept through answer 154 · updated 2026-09-11.**

Consolidated concept material, with current rules and unresolved details distinguished.

## The Bloomkin
Graceful, horned beings formed from living water, the Verdani inhabit Evergrove, a desert realm sustained by an immense underground network of roots and waterways.

## Homeland and settlements
Settlements bloom around oases. Canals wind between gardens and curved sandstone homes.

## Community and everyday life
Healers, waterkeepers, and gardeners sustain communities through water magic and careful stewardship. Life is expressive and communal, with music, shared meals, and festivals. Ancestral memories are preserved in enchanted pools.

## Knowledge traditions from the RPG conversation

Memory Pools tended by Memorykeepers preserve communal experiences and provide a foundation for sharing sightings and contributing knowledge.

## Place in this game

The ancient setting keeps the peoples separate before they have met. The first playable version begins with one Grimnir community; the others are later expansion scope. The developed settlements described above come from the broader world introduction; their precise early forms remain open. Roles are culture-specific callings assigned at adulthood from traits. Their exact roster has not been chosen.

All peoples participate in the shared character system: 27 latent birth traits, playable youth, adult roles and destinies, temporary player inhabitation, and continuing AI life. How reproduction and dissolution appear for each elemental body still needs detailed adaptation.

See [Cultural Knowledge Systems](../05_Knowledge/Shards_Memory_and_Cultural_Hubs.md), [Birth and Inheritance](../03_Characters/Birth_Youth_and_Adulthood.md), and [Ancient Morkahja](../01_World/Ancient_World_and_Magic.md).

All four peoples share a broadly humanoid baseline of ordinary actions, with different elemental abilities layered on top. Baseline embodiment may be represented as underlying traits. All elemental baseline abilities are present by playable youth; the specific action catalogue is still undefined. Knowledge exchanges and social bonding take place through nearby, culturally appropriate interactions. See [Baseline Actions](../03_Characters/Roles_and_Baseline_Abilities.md).
