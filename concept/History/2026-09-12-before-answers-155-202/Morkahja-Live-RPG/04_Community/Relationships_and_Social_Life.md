# Relationships and Social Life

**Current concept through answer 154 · updated 2026-09-11.**

Consolidated concept material, with current rules and unresolved details distinguished.

## Relationships

### Proximity and compatibility

Relationships develop through meaningful interactions between physically nearby characters. Desires match Traits, and Needs match Shards; roles and existing relationship information also shape what is available.

A character who desires Strong can connect with someone who is Strong. An initial greeting or culturally appropriate action can create sympathy shards, enabling further interactions later. Receiving needed information can also deepen the relationship.

### Positive progression

The intended progression is recognition → sympathy → trust, with culturally appropriate bonds developing through repeated matched interactions. These are relationship states or shards rather than a required numeric friendship bar; exact thresholds are not defined.

The first version uses neutral and positive relationships. There is no hatred, rivalry, aversion, or negative reputation simulation. If compatibility is absent, deeper positive interaction simply remains unavailable.

### Community and reproduction

Players create circumstances in which people can meet each other's needs and desires, strengthening the community. Social connection ultimately supports partnership and reproduction and thus population growth. This is an overall social-system purpose, not a rule that every exchange directly causes reproduction.

A character may desire a trait they also possess. Family and parental friends remain a possible starting network; inheritance of specific relationship shards is not defined.

Permanent traits are not erased by ordinary social changes. Temporary relationship knowledge is distinct from the persistent trait pool.

See [Desires](../03_Characters/Identity_Traits_Desires_and_Needs.md) and [Social Gameplay](#social-gameplay).

## Social Gameplay

Roleplay has practical meaning. Shared work, team activity, music, dance, celebration, and communal moments can make people healthier, more resilient, and more grounded in their individuality.

The intended audience is adult, and adult sexuality and intimate relationships were included as part of social life. Their presentation and mechanics are not specified. Early childhood is simulated; youth is the separate playable formation phase, with age-appropriate interaction details still to be defined.

### Contextual interaction

Interactions arise from the actual relationship between two people, their roles, traits, and matching pieces of knowledge. The design should ask what meaningful exchange exists here rather than offer only a shallow universal gesture list.

If one character needs a bear's location and another knows it, a knowledge-sharing action becomes possible. Culture determines how the exchange appears. Other role combinations can unlock special actions for one or both participants.

There is no free player text or proximity voice chat. Signals, contextual actions, movement, and culturally appropriate exchanges carry communication.

Social opportunities require physical proximity and meaningful matching: Desire ↔ Trait or Need ↔ Shard. Executing an interaction can create relationship shards and unlock further bonding, from recognition to sympathy to trust. Neutral and positive states are sufficient; hatred, rivalry, and negative reputation are excluded from the initial design.

Desires extend beyond social life because creatures, objects, and places have traits too. Specific matches should support varied actions, beginning with a manageable cultural interaction library. Ordinary greetings and movement also have a baseline, so not every basic action needs a specialized personal trait. See [Relationships](#relationships) and [Interaction System](../06_Gameplay/Interactions_Discovery_and_Player_Skill.md).

## Knowledge exchange, family cooperation, and unconditional belonging

Exchange Knowledge is a cultural conversation about lived events: what someone made, discovered, or knows about an object's current holder. Information still spreads through performed interactions. Exact Desire–Trait matches preserve the significance of finding a compatible person.

Grimnir affection can include matching a companion's resting resonance. Two willing adults can initiate Reproduction Destinies with individual and shared tasks; a stable nest allows them to travel. Care is not repayment for labor: people belong while young, exhausted, injured, recovering, or unable to work.
