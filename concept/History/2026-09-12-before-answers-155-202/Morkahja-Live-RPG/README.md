## How to read the concept

Each chapter groups related rules and preserves their useful detail. Worked examples explain how those rules meet in play. They are labeled as user-discussion examples, assistant illustrations, or historical/deferred ideas where needed. Creature names, role examples, sample traits, and proposed keybindings are not automatically a finalized content list.

Start with [Game Vision and Principles](00_Core/Game_Vision_and_Principles.md), then [Game Loop and Sessions](00_Core/Game_Loop_and_Sessions.md). The first playable focus is one Grimnir settlement.

The [coverage and structure map](99_Development/Conversation_Coverage_and_Structure_Map.md) links every conversation turn and former file to its current home. [Open Questions](99_Development/Open_Questions.md) and [Ideas and Superseded Proposals](99_Development/Ideas_and_Superseded_Proposals.md) keep unresolved or replaced material out of current rules.

The [complete source conversation](../Morkahja-Concept-Sources.md) is preserved beside this folder and included in the ZIP. Original world-and-peoples lore is retained in the four peoples chapters; its developed cities are distinguished from this RPG's ancient starting period. This remains a local snapshot, not automatic synchronization.
