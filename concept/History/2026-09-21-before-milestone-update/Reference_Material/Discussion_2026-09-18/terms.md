### User

Action or interaction?

### Assistant

**Interaction**.

Use **interaction** as the umbrella term for anything a character does with the world, another character, an object, or themselves.

An **action** is the specific thing performed inside an interaction.

Example:

**Interaction:** Combat  
**Action:** Mountain Strike  
**Target:** Bear  
**Target area:** Claw

So for the Morkahja system: **Interaction → Action → Target → Result**.
