# Roles and Baseline Abilities

**Current concept through answer 251 · updated 2026-09-13.**

Consolidated concept material, with current rules and unresolved details distinguished.

## Social Roles

Roles are culture-specific descriptive titles/flair inferred from dominant current Traits. They supply neither separate abilities nor an extra task/Destiny filter. A hunter tracks because their actual capabilities permit tracking; the label Hunter does not unlock it. Changing Traits may change the appropriate title.

The adulthood rite reveals the first role. Later visits/exchanges at the Memory Fountain update the community's recognition of it. A small player notification may accompany the update; no special change ceremony or stability threshold is established. Exact dominance scoring and title catalogue remain open.

The wider cultures suggest smithing and communal work for Rokur; healing, waterkeeping and gardening for Verdani; learning, architecture and crystal craft for Aevis; forging, navigation, architecture and memorykeeping for Grimnir. These are cultural foundations, not a required class roster. Grimnir communal child-rearing does not establish permanent mother/father gameplay roles.

Independent craft Traits such as Crafter, Leatherworker and Cloak Crafter combine where applicable; they are not ranks. Tasks and actions check these actual qualities alongside Needs, Shards, items and state.

## Baseline Actions

Characters have ordinary embodied actions without requiring a personal trait for every attempt. Broadly humanoid bodies share a common foundation, with different elemental abilities layered on top.

### Availability

All elemental baseline abilities are available when the person becomes playable in youth. Early childhood is simulated rather than player-controlled. This resolves the previous question about waiting for adulthood to unlock the baseline.

Shared humanoid/elemental foundation + personal Traits (including preferences) + equipment + current state/injuries → usable contextual actions. The role label describes the person.

Baseline embodiment can be represented as underlying traits, but no rule deducts it from the 27 personal birth traits. Personal qualities expand possibilities; injury can restrict actions without removing permanent traits.

### Equipment and execution

A character can physically use or wear equipment without a matching build. Compatibility unlocks more of its interaction potential. Equipment-granted capabilities are conditional on the equipment and do not automatically become permanent birth/acquired traits.

Character qualities determine available interactions; player skill determines their execution. Tests vary by interaction rather than always requiring timing.

Seven Grimnir elemental capacities are specified below. Exact specialized actions and physical fitting remain open; primary mouse controls are specified in the presentation and interaction chapters.

See [Equipment and Carrying](../07_Items/Items_Equipment_and_Carrying.md) and [Interaction Discovery](../06_Gameplay/Interactions_Discovery_and_Player_Skill.md).

## Participation beyond the dominant role

A role expresses the dominant trait pattern; it does not limit the person's other traits or assign exclusive normal duties. A Stone-Shaper may hunt if capable, tend children, prepare meals, or gather supplies. The task source considers the whole character, not merely a job title. Broader responsibilities fit the early settlement; narrower specialties may develop later.

Wandering, collecting stories, discovering places, returning Shards, sharing ideas, creativity, and care all contribute. There is no hierarchy that makes productive labor the only legitimate participation. **A Grimnir belongs even when they cannot work.** This includes children, injured or tired people, and stonebound kin receiving care. Role reassignment timing remains unspecified.

Source: [answer 122](../../Morkahja-Concept-Sources.md#turn-dcfbdf7a-f13b-41cc-ba5e-1f7b1c6c75d7).



## Seven shared Grimnir elemental capacities

The new baseline is broad, modest elemental embodiment. All seven capacities belong to Grimnir development; the supplied practical limits are proposals, not numerical balance rules.

1. **Feel vibrations through material.** Contact with stone, metal, or mineral-rich ground conveys nearby movement, strain, and structural differences. Footsteps on a rock shelf and a cracked support are examples. Distant mapping, identifying a source, or interpreting a mechanism needs greater skill. This underlies Gem Seeker Instinct, Stone Whisperers Touch, and parts of Lustrous Gaze.
2. **Recognize useful minerals by contact.** Touch or tasting a prepared ingredient conveys bodily compatibility for growth, repair, and rest. It is not perfect geology or infallible poison/corruption detection.
3. **Shape small amounts of earth and stone.** Smooth an edge, bind loose grains, or patiently form a shallow hollow. Size, hardness, speed, and precision demand more skill; houses, forged blades, and later Skylands remain achievements.
4. **Brace the body and grip stone.** Responsive mineral structure supports carrying, climbing, and enduring knocks. It underlies Mountain Strike and Cliffhanger's Grasp without universal combat mastery, unlimited mass, or immunity to injury.
5. **Incorporate minerals for repair.** Gradually mend minor fractures alongside food, water, and rest. Serious damage needs more care. This grounds Mineral Mend; the old description's reference to healing flesh is not an explanation of ordinary Grimnir self-repair and may belong to a later specialization.
6. **Produce and respond to intentional resonance.** Touch and connected stone carry presence, simple rhythms, and invitations to synchronize. This supports parental care, reassurance, coordination, and sleep. Complex communication and healing need learned skill; innate resonance does not eliminate parenting knowledge.
7. **Settle internal rhythms.** Suitable stone supports rebalancing after exertion or distress; prepared structures improve it. This grounds Heartstone Purification without granting universal protection from curses, corruption, or deep-earth effects.

Personal Traits affect aptitude, strength, sensitivity, and unusual expression. Learned practice develops the capacities into craft, cooking, healing, hunting, engineering, or leadership. Having a baseline does not reveal every specialized interaction in the panel.

Source: [answer 123](../../Morkahja-Concept-Sources.md#turn-17ae976e-2490-486a-b0dc-fe86dd1ab63a).


## Roles follow Traits and communal recognition

A new dominant pattern can change the title when the character visits the fountain. The underlying game can inspect required state without exposing everyone’s Traits to the inhabited player or automatically distributing their learned Shards. The nine named innate capacities supplied in answer 220 and the twelve authored foundations are developed in [First twelve interactions](../06_Gameplay/First_Twelve_Grimnir_Interactions.md). The older seven broad capacities below remain physiological foundations, not a competing final action count.

**Which fit early Grimnir development?**

Almost all have a place in an early society, but several should begin as modest capabilities that later become the named skills above.

| Ability | Place in early Grimnir life | My recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| **Mountain Strike** | Physical defense, breaking obstacles, forceful work. | Innate strength and bodily reinforcement; a powerful, controlled strike requires practice. |
| **Ancestors Muse** | Shared experience, remembrance, inspiration. | An early cultural practice once ancestors and traditions exist. Accessing ancestral wisdom should not be automatic knowledge. |
| **Gem Seeker Instinct** | Finding useful minerals and sensing cavities or fractures. | Strong innate foundation. They sense material properties, rather than knowing that something is “treasure.” |
| **Stone Whisperers Touch** | Feeling the internal structure of stone or metal objects. | Innate sensitivity can come early. Understanding and opening locks is a learned application. |
| **Orator of Bedrock** | Storytelling, reassurance, coordinating communal work. | A naturally resonant voice fits; persuasive mastery develops through experience. |
| **Lustrous Gaze** | Hunting and following movement. | Tracking is an early learned skill supported by their vibration sense. No guaranteed ability to follow any prey. |
| **Heartstone Purification** | Settling disturbed internal rhythms and helping someone recover balance. | Basic self-regulation fits. Removing corruption or harmful magic should require specialized knowledge. |
| **Cliffhanger’s Grasp** | Traveling rocky landscapes and reaching resources. | Strong innate foundation: sensitive grip and sure contact with rock. Difficult climbing still takes skill. |
| **Mineral Mend** | Growth and recovery from bodily damage. | Essential innate self-repair. Healing other people is a further discipline. |
| **Lavastone Culinary** | Preparing nourishing food and mineral mixtures. | Excellent early learned craft. Skyland spices belong to whichever period Skylands become available. |
| **Shadowstone Blend** | Concealment among rocks. | Natural stone appearance supports camouflage. Deliberately suppressing movement or vibration is learned; complete undetectability is too broad. |
| **Grimnirs Aid** | Mutual assistance, communal care, shared labor. | Foundational social behavior. A magical means of calling or strengthening allies would need separate definition. |
