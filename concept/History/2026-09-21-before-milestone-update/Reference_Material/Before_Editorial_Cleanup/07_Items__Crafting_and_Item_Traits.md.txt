# Crafting and Item Traits

**Current concept through answer 251 · updated 2026-09-13.**

How material and tool traits become crafted qualities through flat, overlapping expertise, with deterministic results.

## Physical inputs and predictable outputs

Crafting uses the same trait system as the rest of the world. Materials and tools contribute qualities, while the crafter's relevant traits and the performed interaction determine which qualities reach the finished object.

Results are deterministic: the same relevant person, inputs, tools, and action give a reliable result. There is no random quality or critical-craft roll. Knowledge of the process is therefore useful and dependable.

## Flat, cumulative expertise

Crafter, Leatherworker, and Cloak Crafter are independent qualities. There is no prerequisite ladder from general to specific. Each can be inherited, randomly included at birth, or earned through relevant questing.

All applicable traits contribute together. A character with all three contributes all three when making a leather cloak; possessing one gives that one's contribution. This supersedes the intermediate suggestion of hierarchical specialization. The exact number of input traits preserved per contribution is not fixed.

## Quality means compatibility

A richer object carries more relevant input/tool qualities, such as Soft, Warm, Durable, or Fine. This creates more possible preference Trait or Need matches. A Soft cloak fits someone seeking Soft even if its other qualities are irrelevant to them.

More qualities do not mean a universally better level or larger stat number. The result is useful in more situations and to more people.

## Scarcity and presentation

Clothing requires materials, care, labor, and craft; it is rare and costly in communal effort. It may break and may need repair or replacement. The exact repair rules remain open.

Crafting takes place through world interactions rather than a separate recipe-selection spreadsheet. Discoveries arise through normal play. Detailed recipes, preservation rules, timing, and skill tests remain to be designed.

See [Equipment and Carrying](Items_Equipment_and_Carrying.md) and [Interaction System](../06_Gameplay/Interactions_Discovery_and_Player_Skill.md).

## Example E57: Three relevant qualities contribute

*Illustrative example from the discussion.*

Crafter contributes to crafting generally; Leatherworker contributes when the material is leather; Cloak Crafter contributes when the object is a cloak. A person with all three gets each contribution, without prerequisite ranks or a random quality roll.

## Example E58: A soft cloak is useful to the right person

*Illustrative example from the discussion.*

Inputs and tools can produce Soft, Warm, Durable, and Fine. A person desiring Soft matches because it is soft; other qualities may not matter to them. More qualities create more possible matches. A cloak requested for someone else is a situational task Need, while a personal preference may be a preference Trait.

## Knowledge created by making

Making a spear gives its maker a Shard of the event. That information can be exchanged personally or deposited at the communal source and later retrieved. Crafting thus contributes an object and shareable provenance. Unknown items remain physically usable; additional benefits from identification are a proposal, not a settled trait-reveal rule. See [Items and provenance](Items_Equipment_and_Carrying.md).

Source: [answer 152](../../Morkahja-Concept-Sources.md#turn-bbb2108d-baeb-43ef-b2b8-6ef272b436fa), [answer 154](../../Morkahja-Concept-Sources.md#turn-bbb210ef-cbfb-4fb8-b04f-bf6019f5e780).


## Basic crafting and optional knowledge journeys

Traits, tools, ingredients/materials, and access to the crafting node enable a basic craft without mandatory recipe knowledge. The character's available resources can generate a temporary crafting task with an optional seek-knowledge step.

Example: a character can make a basic spear now or learn how to make a richer one. The fountain checks the Need for a matching Shard. If it lacks the knowledge, prioritize the task with the marker stone; Divine Orientation can lead to an informed person, a cave scroll, or an inscribed stone tablet known to the gods. The fountain's ignorance does not block divine guidance.

Competence transfers to the product: the relevant craft Trait supplies a foundation, while additional knowledge and appropriate material/tool/craft specializations can contribute additional item Traits. Knowing how to work a material or use a tool matters; the output gains more potential compatibility, not universal numerical superiority. Exact contribution counts remain to be defined.


Source: [answer 158](../../Morkahja-Concept-Sources.md#turn-fcfdce63-151d-4c9f-a055-2b3fd0a6dda9).


## Repair is an independent capability

Repair requires a relevant repair Trait, tools, and materials. Knowing how to make an item does not automatically grant repair competence, and a repairer need not be its maker. With all requirements, restore the previous state completely: no repair-quality roll, loss of original qualities, or partial-restoration tax.

A broken weapon creates a Need for repair, like an injured limb creates a Need for healing. If the character cannot fulfill it, prioritize it and follow guidance toward someone or somewhere that can. Item state changes have associated restoration Needs. The analogy between limbs and items is a shared state/Need logic, not a new rule that bodies are transferable inventory objects.


Source: [answer 159](../../Morkahja-Concept-Sources.md#turn-9bd10853-09ea-4c45-b617-df06c5c881d5).


## Grimnir craft language and material culture

Crafting expresses refinement and care: shaping stone, working ore/metal, preparing nourishing minerals and arranging crystal for resonance. Ferrumis supplies a fitting cultural association without conferring a role-based ability. Existing independent craft Traits, optional knowledge improvements, binary equipment condition and full repair rules remain.

Spiral Forge-Ring motifs, visible methods of joining/shaping, and distinctive makers' finishes can make objects feel Grimnir. Keep tooltips grounded in actual Traits and known provenance rather than treating decorative motifs as unchosen bonuses. Anchor crystals, sky-core minerals and the Anchorstone Keystone remain later/specialized references, not automatically available beginner materials. Lavastone Culinary can inform the cooking vocabulary; its historical database entry is not a thirteenth mandatory interaction beside the selected twelve.

Sources and reconciliation: [Grimnir lore source archive](../../Reference_Material/Grimnir_Lore_2026-09-14/README.md).
