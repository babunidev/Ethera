# Verdani

## The Bloomkin
Graceful, horned beings formed from living water, the Verdani inhabit Evergrove, a desert realm sustained by an immense underground network of roots and waterways.

## Homeland and settlements
Settlements bloom around oases. Canals wind between gardens and curved sandstone homes.

## Community and everyday life
Healers, waterkeepers, and gardeners sustain communities through water magic and careful stewardship. Life is expressive and communal, with music, shared meals, and festivals. Ancestral memories are preserved in enchanted pools.

## Knowledge traditions

Memory Pools tended by Memorykeepers preserve communal experiences and provide a foundation for sharing sightings and contributing knowledge.

## Place in the wider world

These communities belong to Morkahja's wider elemental history. The Live RPG begins earlier, with the peoples living separately and the first playable community centered on the Grimnir.

Elemental bodies, cultural knowledge and shared life connect the peoples to the same world of temporary player inhabitation and persistent consequences.
