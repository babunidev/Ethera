# First Playable Scope

The first playable connects the smallest recognizable pieces of Morkahja Live RPG: inhabit a Grimnir, move through a place, meet someone, discover something, bring it home and see an interaction become possible because of the character's qualities and possessions.

## The playable experience

Begin near the Grimnir Memory Fountain. Move a simple figure through a village and into the surrounding land. A few other Grimnir provide opportunities to focus, interact and exchange a greeting. The first greeting can use a temporary small hello bubble for visible feedback.

Find an item and carry or equip it visibly on the body. Discover an interactable landmark, such as a stone circle, gain its knowledge Shard and return to the Fountain to share that knowledge. A tiny set of Traits, preferences, Needs, Shards and items is enough to test that the systems connect.

One or two Traits and a suitable item can make a contextual interaction available. The important demonstration is that the game recognizes the actual requirements and offers the matching interaction. Simple Divine Orientation points toward something relevant in the scene. A climbable tree is another concrete interaction example to explore as this foundation grows.

## Several lives, one architecture

The initial characters share the same structure and each has their own ID and state. They can use identical placeholder figures with slightly varied Traits or matching social preferences.

Leave the inhabited person, return to spectator mode and inhabit another. AI resumes control of the person left behind. Possessions, knowledge, position and tasks continue to belong to that individual. Switching is part of the first playable experience.

## Atmosphere and presentation

An unanimated figure, boxes marked House and lines representing wooden walls are sufficient starting graphics. Simple black-and-white drawings are acceptable. Space, grass, changing wind and natural movement can already suggest the savanna and the quiet beginnings of Grimnir life.

The village and surroundings need readable places to move, discover and interact with. Visual detail and animation can grow alongside the working systems.

## Settlement and world direction

Thirty Grimnir is the working starting population for the settlement, to be refined against resources, roles, tasks and culture. The earliest connected test can use a few simple people.

The intended world grows through large modular hexagonal terrain tiles, initially using a small biome vocabulary such as grass and hills. Tiles contain local places and can take minutes to cross. Forests and mountains tend to continue into adjacent areas. New quest content can expand permanent geography. The first scene should support growth toward that structure.

## Enter an ongoing life

A character normally already has something to do. Adults have available Destiny threads and people have tasks within community life. The player can continue the current task or select another existing thread in the panel. The first playable should make the person's existing state understandable.

## Build in small steps

Establish movement and inhabitation, connect a simple interaction to real requirements, then connect items, discovery and the return of knowledge. Each small addition should work with the same underlying character and world systems.

The [Prototype Readiness Review](Prototype_Readiness_Review.md) checks this experience against the concept and identifies the specific definitions still needed for implementation. [Mountain Strike](../06_Gameplay/Mountain_Strike.md) is a separately specified combat interaction that can later exercise the same framework. The wider twelve-interaction set, archival systems and growing society provide further content for that foundation.
