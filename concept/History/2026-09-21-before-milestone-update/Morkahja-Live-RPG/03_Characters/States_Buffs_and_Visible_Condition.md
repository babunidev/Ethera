# States, Buffs and Visible Condition

Temporary states affect a person's current actions and properties. Swiftness can improve movement, Sharpness can extend view distance, and another effect can widen an interaction's timing window.

## Combining effects

Different effect types coexist and apply together. A stronger or longer-lasting instance of the same effect replaces or refreshes the existing instance. Action availability remains binary even when several sources enable it; explanations can list every contributing Trait, item or state.

## Community effects and outings

People physically present in the village can receive the effect of a social event, including those who did not directly participate. More compatible participants extend the effect's duration.

Travel-related duration is measured in outings. A one-use effect lasts through departure and adventure, then is consumed immediately on re-entering the settlement. Effects with several outing uses lose one on each return and end when their uses run out. Even a short trip counts on return.

## Visible condition

Posture, gestures, movement, clothing, health and energy communicate how people are doing. When several states affect visible behavior, the oldest active state appears first.

For example, someone becomes Tired and later Injured. Their tired behavior remains visible until rest resolves it, after which the injury becomes the visible condition. Both states affect mechanics throughout their active time, and both appear in the panel.
