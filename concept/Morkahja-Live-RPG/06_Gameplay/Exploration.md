# Exploration

The world rewards attention. A landscape can seem quiet and sparse until movement, sound or a relevant character quality reveals something within it.

## Notice and investigate

A rabbit leaves cover, a bird moves, grass bends, or light glints between stones. A falling leaf can draw the eye to a patch of ground; sunlight through glass can reveal an object. Hover confirms an interaction opportunity, and deliberate focus opens its available actions.

The player learns this environmental language through travel. A bear's appearance becomes a meaningful encounter: observe, keep distance, leave or engage through fitting interactions.

## A character's perspective

Traits shape discovery. Keen perception can reveal a small detail, while climbing capability opens a route into a tree or onto a rocky shelf. The person discovers interactions through their experiences and retains those discoveries in their panel.

Personal knowledge records what this character has observed or learned. A player's familiarity with a place can differ from the knowledge of the life they currently inhabit.

## Direction and discovery

Divine Orientation gives intermittent signs toward the next useful step. Wind, light, birds and elemental resonance can guide attention. Milestone 3 first implements a Ground Wind-Wave and a Ground Glint toward the reserved objective. Nearby opportunities can draw interest even while another task is active.

The player decides what to investigate and whether to change priorities. Journeys combine a direction with room for encounters and discovery along the way.

## An expanding landscape

Large hexagonal terrain tiles contain many local places. Each Destiny start adds one authored puzzle-piece hexagon whose touching edges match its neighbors. Forest and mountain — and river — continue across those matched edges. A tile remains part of the world after its Destiny Quest ends.

Familiar caves, shrines and trails can gain new contents and purposes, keeping return journeys meaningful. See [World Nodes](../01_World/World_Nodes_and_Generation.md).

## Bringing something back

Goods require physical carrying, while reports become Shards that can be exchanged or contributed to communal memory. A useful observation can make a journey valuable on its own.

A traveler notices something glittering in a tree. Climbing reveals the object and a view of a nearby landmark. On returning, the person brings both the object and a report that another inhabitant can use.

## Discovery through attention

Hover an on-screen interactable landmark to acquire its discovery Shard automatically. The record identifies the character and landmark, Action Discovery, Reaction None, Result Discovered, place and world time. A discovered stone circle can then be shared with the Memory Fountain through Echo Imprint. Discovery enters the playable sequence in Milestone 2.
