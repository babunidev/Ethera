# Game Loop and Sessions

The immediate rhythm is **move → observe → notice → interpret → interact → continue**. A session begins by inhabiting an available person and joining their ongoing situation.

## Entering a life

Character selection introduces the person's expressed qualities, current circumstances and available work. Youths are beginning to discover themselves; adults have an unfolding Destiny. Choosing a life establishes the context for the session.

The inhabited character usually already has a task. The player can continue it or select another existing task or quest on the stone board. One priority stone determines the focus of Divine Orientation.

## Exploring and acting

Travel reveals details: a sound in a bush, a bird, a glint, tracks, a useful object or another traveler. Character Traits and condition shape what can be noticed and which interactions become available.

The player approaches people and objects, chooses a contextual action and performs its skill phase. An encounter can lead to knowledge exchange, craft, a relationship, combat, assistance or further preparation. Failed attempts can create recovery Needs and change the route forward.

## Knowledge and purpose

The cultural knowledge source supplies relevant information and suitable community work through performed interactions. Tasks describe their requirements; missing information sends the character to the source. Divine Orientation then supports the next useful step through subtle environmental signs.

The common task area holds several personal and community tasks. Players choose which to pursue. Important new Needs draw attention through a glow or pulse while the current selection remains active. When a completed thread needs a replacement, the default is the oldest personal Need, followed by suitable community work.

## Returning

Travelers bring physical goods, useful reports and experience back to the settlement. Deliveries support community activity; sharing Shards broadens communal knowledge. Meals, rest, care and celebration restore the person and connect the adventure to village life.

Some village effects last for a number of outings. Returning to the settlement consumes an outing use, linking community events to the journeys they support.

## Leaving

Manual logout places the character under AI control and sends them home. An unexpected disconnect begins with a waiting period and can offer a temporary stand-in. The person's tasks and history remain in the world.

## Across generations

Community support → shared creation → simulated early childhood → playable youth → participation and self-discovery → adulthood rite → Destiny Quests and Knots → goods, knowledge and conditions for future people.

Only players advance personal Destiny and initiate reproduction. AI supports ordinary life while the simulation runs.

## A short session

A player inhabits someone seeking a plant in the woods. Along the way, they investigate a glint and meet a traveler with a useful report. They return with the plant and a new sighting. The goods and knowledge have value even though the person's larger Destiny continues into another session.
