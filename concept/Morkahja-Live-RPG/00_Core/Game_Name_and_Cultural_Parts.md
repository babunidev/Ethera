# Game Name and Cultural Parts

**Ethera: The Stonewrought** is the current title. **Ethera** names the game, and **The Stonewrought** describes its current chapter, focused on the Grimnir. The world remains **Morkahja** throughout the chapters.

## Cultural structure

| Working title | People |
| --- | --- |
| Ethera: The Stonewrought | Grimnir |
| Ethera: Bloomkin | Verdani |
| Ethera: Windweavers | Aevis |
| Ethera: Embersworn | Rokur |

Each chapter takes place in the same world of Morkahja. The later cultural titles above remain working names. Each cultural part develops a people's society, environment, interactions, beliefs, roles and elemental way of life. The Grimnir begin the playable experience; later parts expand the same larger game.

## Naming character

Ethera draws its sound and connection to the setting from Ethera's Vale. The title has room for the peoples' different histories and for a world that grows through their lives. Stonewrought evokes shaping, craft and continuing transformation, fitting both Grimnir material nature and the early grounded community.

The cultural subtitle remains useful as Grimnir life develops toward later forms of civilization. The naming discussion and further subtitle ideas remain in development history.
