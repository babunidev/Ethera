# Game Vision and Principles

**Ethera: The Stonewrought** is the title of the current Grimnir chapter of **Ethera**, set in the world of **Morkahja**. [Game Name and Cultural Parts](Game_Name_and_Cultural_Parts.md) describes the naming structure.

Ethera: The Stonewrought is a persistent shared world whose people can be temporarily inhabited by players. Each character already has a body, abilities, preferences, relationships, knowledge and a place in the community. A session continues that person's life.

The experience is quiet, curious and dreamlike. Small discoveries, meaningful encounters and shared work give a session its shape. Players explore, exchange knowledge, make things, form relationships and pursue Destiny. Their contributions remain with the people and world they leave behind.

## A world of continuing lives

The same individual lives under AI control between player sessions. Their possessions, development and unfinished tasks stay with them. Another player may later continue the life from its changed circumstances.

Progress belongs to the character and community. A successful hunt supplies the village; a discovered resource helps future travelers; a new Trait broadens one person's possibilities. Player accounts carry no gameplay possessions or power between lives.

The settlement provides a secure foundation for its people. Player activity brings flourishing: richer culture, surplus materials, meaningful relationships, new children and the means to support them. AI maintains ordinary life while players advance personal Destiny.

## The first community

Play begins with a Grimnir settlement in an ancient age of Morkahja. The people have recently awakened into individual lives and are learning to build a shared culture. The other elemental peoples inhabit the wider world in their own separate communities.

The player acts from within one life. Construction, task needs and settlement placement arise through the community simulation, with people contributing through their capabilities and actions.

## Principles

1. **Inhabit a person.** Identity, history and progress persist with the world's characters.
2. **Keep sessions voluntary.** A brief visit can make a lasting contribution, and a larger undertaking can continue across sessions.
3. **Build possibilities through Traits.** Permanent binary qualities broaden what a person can do.
4. **Connect lives through Needs.** Existing people, places, knowledge and objects bring adventures together.
5. **Keep purpose stable and routes responsive.** A search for a bear claw can continue after the bear dies and someone takes the claw.
6. **Preserve physical continuity.** Items, geography and consequences survive the tasks that first made them important.
7. **Expand and reuse the world.** New terrain and content coexist with familiar places that gain new purposes.
8. **Let capability and skill work together.** Character qualities determine available actions; the player performs each action's challenge.
9. **Express information through the world.** Bodies, movement, light, sound and contextual interfaces make situations readable.
10. **Make community matter.** Work, care, knowledge, celebration and relationships sustain shared life.
11. **Give exploration room to breathe.** Intermittent signs and small discoveries reward attention.
12. **Communicate through actions.** Cultural exchanges, gestures and cooperation carry player interaction; player text and voice chat are absent.

## Persistence

The world runs across participating peers. While simulation is active, AI carries on ordinary life. When active simulation stops, the saved world holds its state until it resumes.
