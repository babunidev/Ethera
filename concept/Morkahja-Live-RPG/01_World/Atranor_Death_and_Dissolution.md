# Atranor, Death and Dissolution

Individuality is sustained through Essence, purpose and connection. Atranor is the place of divine judgment where a life may be renewed or reach its end.

## The judgment

At Atranor, the player faces a challenge suited to the character's current capabilities. A labyrinth, memory challenge or knowledge problem can express the trial, with Combat, Trickery, Aid and Social offering appropriate approaches.

The challenge provides an attainable solution through the person's capabilities. Its outcome depends on the player's execution.

## Continuation

Success restores the same individual and their carried possessions at the nearest appropriate safe sacred place. A stone circle, shrine, small temple or other god-linked site can provide that return point. Life continues from there.

## Final dissolution

Failure ends the individual permanently. Their personal Needs, quests and Destiny progress end with them. Unfinished community work returns to the cultural source for reassignment.

Carried items drop and remain in the world. Already shared knowledge, completed work and other consequences continue to matter. The person's Essence can return to the world's elemental life, while their completed life remains part of its history.

## Physical defeat

Injury changes the available actions in an encounter. A person too injured to continue fighting may still crawl away, seek help, surrender, accept capture, reach safety or perform another fitting survival action. The resulting circumstances determine what follows.

## Grimnir stonebound recovery

Prolonged life deep underground can gradually draw a Grimnir toward primal stone. Movement slows, their body becomes heavier and bulkier, and awareness turns inward. Ordinary cave visits and surface rest remain part of healthy life.

While a trace of individuality remains and the person wishes to return, care and restored elemental balance can recover awareness and movement. Longer stonebound residence calls for longer recovery.

## Memory and legacy

Grimnir tradition preserves ancestral echoes through memory-stone rituals. Stories, experiences and traces of craft can remain in archives, structures and later Skyland cores. Such records carry a person's influence forward through the community.

## Corruption

Corruption distorts elemental relationships. The Grimnir form associated with this distortion is the Crumblegheist: erosion, haunted instability and loss of structural coherence. Its presence belongs to Morkahja's wider history of disturbed magic.
