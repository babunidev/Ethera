# Twelve foundations from Grimnir capacities

**Current concept through answer 251 · updated 2026-09-13.**


## Twelve foundations from Grimnir capacities

The first content set is twelve interactions, three in each of Combat, Trickery, Aid and Social. All are authored individually with their own complete requirements, skill phase, animation, timing, sound and results. Their names and characteristics should grow from innate Grimnir capacities rather than generic menu verbs.

| Category | Working name | Selected function |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Combat | Mountain Strike | Basic unarmed attack using the mineral body. |
| Combat | Stoneborn Resilience | Active defense through bodily reinforcement. |
| Combat | Earthshaping | Craft a simple stone/mineral weapon. |
| Trickery | Cliffhanger’s Grasp | Climb/traverse suitable rock. |
| Trickery | Stone Sense | Track movement and physical disturbances. |
| Trickery | Mineral Affinity | Recognize/assemble useful materials into a survival kit. |
| Aid | Mineral Mend | Heal an injury using suitable mineral material. |
| Aid | Inner Harmony | Replenish Essence and stabilize individuality/internal condition. |
| Aid | Elemental Sustenance | Feed/nourish, including culinary preparation. |
| Social | Echo Attunement | Cultural knowledge exchange, especially with memory stones. |
| Social | Resonant Touch | Resonance music and shared rhythmic practice. |
| Social | Living Stone Adornment | Body shaping: lasting bodily enhancement. |

Exchange Knowledge can be available when either participant has knowledge matching the other’s Need; both sides do not always need to offer knowledge simultaneously. The authored interaction and player participation still matter.

The twelve functions were selected by the user in answer 219. Answer 221 asks to map the existing nine names and invent three missing ones; the table is the assistant's resulting working mapping. Mountain Strike is then developed explicitly by the user. Elemental Sustenance and Living Stone Adornment, and the precise one-to-one mapping of the remaining functions, remain working content names rather than independently ratified final names. Recall Knowledge is an additional contextual inspection interaction; its exact relationship to Echo Attunement is not settled.

The original nine named capacities are Stone Sense (vibrations, cracks and strain through stone/metal), Mineral Affinity (nourishment and growth minerals), Earthshaping (slow touch/vibration shaping), Stoneborn Resilience (reinforcement), Cliffhanger’s Grasp (rock grip), Mineral Mend (gradual mineral repair with nourishment/rest), Resonant Touch (presence, comfort and developing life through vibration), Inner Harmony (settling rhythms in rest), and Echo Attunement (preserved memories and ancestral impressions, understood through practice and guidance). The last is not automatic universal ancestral knowledge.

Baseline capacity does not mean all specialized actions are always available. Each concrete action still checks its own recipe. A later Spear Thrust is a distinct authored interaction with its own weapon, body and target requirements, animation and execution challenge; it is not merely an unexamined Mountain Strike reskin.

Body shaping creates a lasting enhancement, compared with enchanting or socketing a gem on the body. It is not the temporary buff once suggested for social shaping. Exact slots, limits, reversibility and effects need design.

Replenishing Essence is prayer-like aid connected to the gods, sustaining mind, motivation and individuality. Aimless wandering can drain Essence; aid from a companion or village helps restore it. This complements safe basic village life and Atranor rather than replacing them. Rates, depletion triggers and the precise distinction from rest/nourishment remain open.

Earlier candidate actions include Inspire/Encourage, Affection/Flirt and a possible stealing interaction. They are not in the chosen first twelve: replenishment replaces inspiration, body shaping replaces flirting. This does not forbid appropriate affection in wider social life. Crafting can appear in multiple categories; the categories describe authored content, not separate crafting engines.

## Relationship to the historical ability catalogue

The direct Grimnir Works archive confirms the progression from twelve older database abilities, through seven proposed broad capacities, to nine named innate tooltips. The Live RPG then uses those foundations for its chosen twelve authored functions. These are three different lists serving different purposes, not conflicting counts to merge.

Use Stone Sense, Mineral Affinity, Earthshaping, Stoneborn Resilience, Cliffhanger's Grasp, Mineral Mend, Resonant Touch, Inner Harmony and Echo Attunement as the established innate vocabulary. Historical names such as Lavastone Culinary, Heartstone Purification, Gem Seeker Instinct, Orator of Bedrock and Ancestors Muse remain available as specialized/cultural reference. Do not automatically import their broad promises of invisibility, infallible tracking or mastery into baseline abilities.

Sources and reconciliation: [Grimnir lore source archive](../../Reference_Material/Grimnir_Lore_2026-09-14/README.md).
