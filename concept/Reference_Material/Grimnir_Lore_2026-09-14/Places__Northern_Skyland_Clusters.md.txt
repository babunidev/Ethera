# Northern Skyland Clusters

**Type:** Grimnir Skyland region  
**Associated people:** [Grimnir](../Races/Grimnir.md)  
**Parent location:** [Grimnir Skylands](Grimnir_Skylands.md)

The Northern Skyland Clusters are conservative Grimnir communities known for preserving ancient forging methods and traditional resonance patterns. Their architecture likely favors monumental stability, ancestral continuity, and carefully maintained craft lineages.

## Lore Hooks

- Stronghold of Anchor philosophy.
- Keepers of ancient forge-schools and old resonance patterns.
- Potential tension with the more experimental Convergence Core.
